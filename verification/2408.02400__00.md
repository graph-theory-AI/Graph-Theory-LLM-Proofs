## Caveats

- For k >= 6 the parameters are established by the (fully verified) induction, not by
  direct computation; this is a normal state of affairs for an infinite family, and
  the base case and first inductive step were both verified independently by machine.
- The writeup's remark that the construction "also gives such a graph for k = 4" is
  true (the seed itself) but not new — it is an instance of Steiner's Theorem 1.4.
- The lemma's equality direction needs the independent-part hypothesis; the writeup
  states this and discharges it at every step (seed partition has I = {8,9};
  persistence proven). Without it only zeta(mu(H)) >= zeta(H)+1 would follow, which
  would still give zeta(G_k) >= k but not equality; no such gap arises here.
- Edge/convention checks: the Mycielskian convention used (root adjacent to the
  shadow layer only) is the standard one and is self-contained in the writeup; the
  identity omega(mu(H)) = max(omega(H), 2) is applied only with omega = 4, where it
  is safe; k >= 5 is the range demanded by Problem 1.5 and is fully covered.
- The writeup's own caveat is only bibliographic priority; my search found no prior
  resolution of Problem 1.5, so the result appears to be new, though a definitive
  novelty claim would require the usual human literature diligence.
