## Interpretation

The catalog statement is the parenthetical remark immediately after Conjecture 1.3 of
arXiv:2204.10119 (Chudnovsky, Scott, Seymour, Spirkl, *Bipartite graphs with no
$K_6$ minor*). I fetched the paper (v1 PDF) and confirmed the sentence verbatim on
page 2:

> "(Indeed, as far as we know, every non-null graph with minimum degree at least six
> and maximum degree at most eight has a $K_6$ minor.)"

immediately following Conjecture 1.3 ("*Every non-null 6-regular graph has a $K_6$
minor.*"). Crucially, the sentence just before Conjecture 1.3 reads: "We have found
$K_6$-minor-free graphs with minimum degree six and maximum degree at most nine; and
$K_6$-minor-free graphs with minimum degree five, maximum degree seven, and average
degree arbitrarily close to 98/15." So the authors themselves delimit the open
window: min degree 6 with max degree 8 was, to their knowledge, enough to force a
$K_6$ minor, while max degree 9 is not.

The writeup formalizes the statement exactly as intended: a non-null finite simple
graph, minimum degree $\ge 6$, maximum degree $\le 8$, claimed to force a $K_6$
minor; it exhibits a 28-vertex counterexample with degree sequence $6^{22}7^48^2$
(i.e. degrees only 6, 7, 8). This is not interpretation gaming: the parenthetical is
a universally quantified statement about all such graphs, and a single counterexample
refutes it in the sense the authors intended ("as far as we know" signals an open
belief, which this construction settles negatively). The writeup is also careful
about scope: it explicitly does **not** claim to refute Conjecture 1.3 (6-regular;
the counterexample has vertices of degree 7 and 8), Conjecture 1.4 (min degree
$\ge 7$), or any 5-/6-connected strengthening (the counterexample has a $K_4$
separator, hence connectivity 4 — consistent with Jørgensen's conjecture and with
the known results on large 6-connected graphs).

Interpretation verdict: **fair and intended**.

## Caveats

1. **What is refuted.** The refuted statement is the parenthetical "as far as we
   know" belief, exactly as extracted by the catalog — not the paper's formal
   Conjecture 1.3 (6-regular), Conjecture 1.4 (min degree $\ge 7$), or Jørgensen's
   conjecture, all of which remain open and are untouched by this construction (the
   writeup says this itself).
2. **Connectivity.** $G$ has a $K_4$ separator (connectivity 4), so any strengthened
   version of the belief demanding 5- or 6-connectivity survives; the writeup states
   this caveat explicitly.
3. **Lemma 2 statement nit.** As stated, Lemma 2 should require $S$ to be a clique
   in both $Q_1$ and $Q_2$ (its proof uses edges of $S$ inside $Q_1$). In the
   application $S$ induces a $K_4$ in each copy, so the requirement is met; no
   repair is needed for the result.
4. **No exact minor test on $G$.** $K_6$-minor-freeness of $G$ was not established
   by an independent exhaustive algorithm (infeasible at $n=28$); it rests on the
   two hand-verified textbook lemmas plus machine-certified planarity, corroborated
   by a validated heuristic search. I consider this a complete verification, not a
   gap.
5. **Novelty.** No prior publication of a min-degree-6 / max-degree-8
   $K_6$-minor-free graph was found in searches, and the source paper's authors
   explicitly only knew max-degree-9 examples. If an unpublished example existed in
   folklore, the verdict would shift to ALREADY_KNOWN, but nothing indexed supports
   that.
6. **Conventions.** All graphs finite and simple, as the source paper requires
   ("All graphs in this paper are finite and have no loops or parallel edges");
   the amalgam was checked to be simple and connected, and "non-null" is satisfied.

## Referee summary

I set out to break this proof and could not. The construction is elegant and
completely explicit: modify the icosahedron by two face insertions, an edge flip,
and a third insertion to plant a $K_4$ whose degree vector $(3,5,5,7)$ is
self-complementary under the pairing $x\leftrightarrow a$, add an apex of degree
only 8 over the eight untouched vertices, and glue two copies along the $K_4$ so
that all four low-degree vertices are healed to degree 7. Every numerical and
structural claim survives independent machine verification; the two supporting
lemmas (apex graphs are $K_6$-minor-free; $\le 4$-clique amalgams preserve
$K_6$-minor-freeness) are standard and were re-proved line by line; the quoted
conjecture matches the source paper verbatim; and a validated heuristic minor
search finds no $K_6$ model. The result refutes the parenthetical belief of
Chudnovsky, Scott, Seymour, and Spirkl in its intended sense, while leaving their
formal Conjectures 1.3 and 1.4 open. Verdict: CONFIRMED, high confidence.
