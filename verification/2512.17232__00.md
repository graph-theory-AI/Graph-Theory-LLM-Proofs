# Referee report: 2512.17232__00 (Conjecture 6, "An Induced A-Path Theorem")

## Interpretation

The catalog statement is Conjecture 6 of arXiv:2512.17232**v1** (Hickingbotham–Joret,
Dec 2025), which I re-extracted verbatim from the arXiv v1 HTML:

> For every $k\in\mathbb{N}$, every graph $G$ and $X,Y\subseteq V(G)$ satisfies one of
> the following: (i) $G$ contains $k$ pairwise anti-complete $(X,Y)$-paths; or (ii)
> there exists a set $Z\subseteq V(G)$ with $|Z|\leqslant k-1$ such that $G-N_G[Z]$
> has no $(X,Y)$-path.

The paper's definitions (also checked verbatim in v1): an $(X,Y)$-path is a path
$P=v_1,\dots,v_m$ with $V(P)\cap X=\{v_1\}$ and $V(P)\cap Y=\{v_m\}$; two paths are
anti-complete if they are vertex-disjoint and no edge of $G$ joins them. The writeup
uses exactly these definitions; its canonical paths $P_i=x_ia_ib_iy_i$ satisfy the
strict $(X,Y)$-path definition, and its impossibility argument ("no two anti-complete
paths") is proved for *all* simple $X\to Y$ paths, a superset of $(X,Y)$-paths, so no
convention mismatch can save the conjecture. The interpretation is the intended one,
not a strawman: a $k=2$ counterexample (packing $1$, minimum radius-1 separator $2$)
falsifies the universally quantified v1 statement.

Would a reasonable author consider their conjecture resolved by this? **They already
have, in the same direction.** On 2026-09-01 (two days before this review) the authors
posted v2 of the paper, in which Conjecture 6 is weakened to $|Z|\leqslant k$, with the
new remark (verbatim from v2 HTML):

> The bound $|Z|\leqslant k$ would be best possible if we require our hitting sets to
> be radius-$1$ balls. Indeed, Albrechtsen, Huynh, Jacobs, Knappe, and Wollan [4]
> constructed a graph $G$ with sets $X,Y\subseteq V(G)$ such that $G$ contains no two
> anti-complete $(X,Y)$-paths, yet $G-N_G[v]$ contains an $(X,Y)$-path for every
> $v\in V(G)$.

That is precisely the phenomenon exhibited by the writeup's graph. So the v1 bound
$k-1$ is genuinely false — but the falsification was already known.

## Reference check

- **Conjecture 6 (v1) statement.** Re-extracted verbatim from
  https://arxiv.org/html/2512.17232v1 — matches the catalog extraction exactly
  ($|Z|\leqslant k-1$). The definitions of $(X,Y)$-path and anti-complete used by the
  writeup match the paper's. Confirmed.
- **The writeup cites no external theorems** — the disproof is self-contained.
  Nothing to falsify on that front.
- **Prior literature (found by this review, decisive for the verdict).**
  S. Albrechtsen, T. Huynh, R. W. Jacobs, P. Knappe, P. Wollan, *A Menger-type theorem
  for two induced paths*, SIAM J. Discrete Math. 38(2):1438–1450, 2024
  (arXiv:2305.04721). Their **Lemma 5** (verbatim from arXiv HTML): "For every integer
  $d\geq 2$, there exists a graph $G$ and $X,Y\subseteq V(G)$ such that there do not
  exist two disjoint $X$–$Y$ paths $P_1,P_2$ such that $\mathrm{dist}_G(P_1,P_2)\geq d$,
  nor does there exist a vertex $z\in V(G)$ such that $B_G(z,2d-3)$ intersects every
  $X$–$Y$ path." The $d=2$ case (their Figure 1, with $|X|=|Y|=3$) is exactly a graph
  with no two anti-complete $(X,Y)$-paths in which no single closed neighbourhood hits
  all $(X,Y)$-paths — i.e., the $k=2$ refutation of Conjecture 6 (v1). This *predates*
  the conjecture (the source paper even cites AHJKW as [4] for other purposes, so the
  v1 bound $k-1$ was an oversight).
- **Source paper v2** (posted 2026-09-01, after the attack): Conjecture 6 is revised to
  $|Z|\leqslant k$ and the AHJKW construction is explicitly credited as showing the v1
  bound fails. The main theorem's constant also improved ($78(k-1)\to 54(k-1)$).
- No indexed paper crediting anyone else with refuting the v1 bound was found; the
  correction appears only via AHJKW's 2024 lemma and the v2 revision.

## Computational check

Script: `verification/scripts/2512.17232__00/verify.py`
(Python 3 + networkx; runs in seconds). Independent re-encoding of the construction
from the writeup's prose. Results:

- Gadget: $n=16$, $m=18$; $X$, $Y$ disjoint stable sets; the four stated closed
  neighbourhoods $N[x_0]$, $N[y_0]$, $N[a_0]$, $N[b_0]$ all match the writeup.
- **88** simple $X\to Y$ paths (identical under the strict $(X,Y)$-path definition,
  since all $x_i,y_i$ have degree 1); **0** anti-complete pairs among all
  $\binom{88}{2}=3828$ pairs. Max anti-complete packing $=1$.
- Exhaustive search over all $Z$ with $|Z|\leqslant 1$ (17 candidates): none makes
  $G-N[Z]$ $(X,Y)$-path-free. $Z=\{a_0,b_0\}$ succeeds. Minimum separator $=2$.
- Disjoint union of 2 copies ($n=32$, 176 paths): max anti-complete packing $=2$;
  exhaustive search over all $Z$ with $|Z|\leqslant 3$ (5,489 candidates): none works;
  the size-4 set $\{a_0,b_0\}$-per-copy works. So for $k=3$: packing $2<3$, minimum
  separator $4>3$.

All of the writeup's claimed numbers reproduce exactly. No computational discrepancy.

## Caveats

1. **Timeline.** The attack targeted the catalog snapshot of Conjecture 6 = the v1
   statement ($|Z|\leqslant k-1$), which was the live statement when the catalog was
   auto-reviewed (2026-05-14, "open, high confidence"). The v2 revision
   ($|Z|\leqslant k$) appeared 2026-09-01. The writeup genuinely disproves the target
   as posed; it is the *catalog's* "open" status that was wrong — AHJKW's Lemma 5
   (2024) already refuted it, and the auto-review missed this.
2. **Novelty of the gadget, not of the result.** The writeup's 16-vertex graph appears
   to be an independent construction (AHJKW's Figure 1 example uses $|X|=|Y|=3$ and is
   different), but the mathematical content — packing 1, radius-1 separator 2 — is the
   same known fact.
3. **The scaling remark overshoots even the v2 revision.** The verified disjoint-union
   bound (min separator $=2(k-1)$ with packing $k-1$) exceeds the *revised* v2 bound
   $|Z|\leqslant k$ for every $k\geqslant 3$, since $2(k-1)>k$. v2 quantifies over
   "every graph $G$", so disjoint unions are admissible; unless the authors intend a
   connectivity or asymptotic caveat, the v2 conjecture as literally stated is also
   false for $k\geqslant 3$ by two copies of the (already cited) AHJKW gadget. This is
   a finding worth communicating to the source authors, but it is outside the refereed
   claim.
4. **Edge cases.** $k=1$ is unaffected (both the writeup and the conjecture are fine
   there); a $k=2$ counterexample suffices against the universally quantified
   statement. $\mathbb{N}$-convention ($0\in\mathbb{N}$ or not) is immaterial. The
   strict vs loose $(X,Y)$-path definition is immaterial (checked both). The writeup's
   caveat line ("works with disjoint stable terminal sets and with induced or
   terminal-clean paths") is accurate: $X,Y$ are disjoint stable sets, the $P_i$ are
   induced, and the non-packing argument covers all path notions.
5. Nothing is over-claimed in the verdict block relative to what is proven; the
   one-line summary matches the verified content.

## Referee summary

The writeup is mathematically correct in every step: the 16-vertex graph has no two
anti-complete $(X,Y)$-paths yet requires two closed-neighbourhood centres to destroy
all $(X,Y)$-paths, refuting Conjecture 6 of arXiv:2512.17232v1 ($|Z|\leqslant k-1$) at
$k=2$; every claim, including the $2(k-1)$ disjoint-union scaling, was reproduced by
exhaustive computation. However, the refutation is not new: Albrechtsen, Huynh, Jacobs,
Knappe and Wollan (SIAM J. Discrete Math. 2024, Lemma 5, $d=2$) had already constructed
such a graph, and Hickingbotham and Joret revised Conjecture 6 to $|Z|\leqslant k$ in
arXiv v2 (2026-09-01) citing exactly that construction. Verdict: ALREADY_KNOWN (with
the side observation that the verified scaling remark also refutes the v2-revised bound
for $k\geqslant 3$).
