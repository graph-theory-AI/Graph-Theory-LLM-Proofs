# Referee report: 2004.07214__00

**Target claim.** Conjecture 7.1 of arXiv:2004.07214 (Bonamy, Defrain, Micek,
Nourine): *"For every t, there is an output-polynomial time algorithm for
Dom-Enum in incomparability graphs of S_t-free posets."* The writeup claims a
proof, in the stronger form of an `n^{O(t)}`-delay, polynomial-space
enumeration algorithm, via three ingredients: (1) any linear extension of an
S_t-free poset is a linear mim-width ≤ t−1 layout of its incomparability
graph; (2) a self-contained bounded-lmim-width dynamic program deciding
existence of a minimal dominating set with prescribed inclusions/exclusions;
(3) binary flashlight search with that extension oracle.

## Interpretation

I fetched arXiv:2004.07214v2 (the 26 Nov 2025 revision of the Discrete
Mathematics 2025 paper) and read Sections 1, 2 and 7 in full.

- Conjecture 7.1 is stated verbatim as in the catalog: "For every t, there is
  an output-polynomial time algorithm for Dom-Enum in incomparability graphs
  of S_t-free posets."
- The paper's definition of S_t (Section 2, Figure 2): "the standard example
  of order t" is the poset with bipartition A = {a_1,…,a_t},
  B = {b_1,…,b_t} such that a_i < b_j for all i ≠ j; "S_t-free" means no
  induced suborder isomorphic to S_t (the paper defines P[X] as the induced
  suborder, and dimension monotone "under taking induced suborders"). This is
  exactly the writeup's formalization. No interpretation gaming: the writeup
  attacks precisely the intended conjecture, and proves the strongest natural
  reading (polynomial delay, polynomial space, for every fixed t), which any
  reasonable author would accept as resolving the conjecture affirmatively.
- The surrounding Section 7 text confirms the context claimed in the prompt:
  "The algorithm provided by Corollary 5.3 covers in fact all comparability
  graphs of S_t-free posets… To match Theorem 1.2, it is tempting to wonder
  what happens for incomparability graphs of S_t-free posets. Our proof
  clearly does not extend naturally, as it crucially relies on the bounded
  dimension representation—recall that not all S_t-free posets have small
  dimension [Tro92]."

Interpretation is the intended one: **interpretation_ok = true**.

## Reference check

The writeup is deliberately self-contained (it proves its own DP "rather than
invoking a metatheorem") and cites no external theorems, so nothing it *uses*
can be misquoted. I checked instead (a) the source-paper claims it relies on,
and (b) the literature it *should* have cited:

1. **arXiv:2004.07214v2** (fetched, full PDF read): Conjecture 7.1, the
   definition of S_t, Theorems 1.1/1.2, Corollary 5.3's coverage of
   comparability graphs of S_t-free posets, and Conjectures 7.2/7.3 are all as
   described. The Nov 2025 revision still lists Conjecture 7.1 as open.
2. **Kang, Kwon, Strømme, Telle, "A width parameter useful for chordal and
   co-comparability graphs", TCS 704:1–17 (2017), arXiv:1606.08087** (fetched,
   full text extracted). **Proposition 3.6**: given a co-comparability graph
   G, a co-comparability ordering (computable in O(|V|+|E|) time by
   McConnell–Spinrad) yields a *linear* branch-decomposition of *linear*
   mim-width at most t−1 whenever G is (K_t ⊟ K_t)-free, for t ≥ 2 — where
   K_t ⊟ K_t is two t-cliques joined by a perfect matching. A co-comparability
   ordering is exactly a linear extension of the associated poset, and by the
   writeup's own (correct) Section 5, (K_t ⊟ K_t)-free co-comparability graphs
   are exactly the incomparability graphs of S_t-free posets. **So Lemma 1 —
   the writeup's self-declared "main new observation" — is a published 2017
   result, proved there by the same induced-matching-to-two-cliques
   argument.** Kang et al. even derive (σ,ρ)-domination consequences
   (their Theorem 1.1 / Corollary 3.7, e.g. Minimum Dominating Set in
   O(n^{3t+4})).
3. **Golovach, Heggernes, Kanté, Kratsch, Sæther, Villanger,
   "Output-Polynomial Enumeration on Graphs of Bounded (Local) Linear
   MIM-Width", Algorithmica 80(2):714–741 (2018), arXiv:1509.03753**
   (confirmed): all minimal dominating sets of a graph given with a layout of
   bounded linear mim-width can be enumerated with polynomial (indeed linear)
   delay and polynomial space. This is the writeup's Lemmas 2–4 as a
   metatheorem; the source paper itself cites this as [GHK+18] (for
   d-trapezoid graphs), and Kang et al. explicitly mention it.
4. Consequence of 2+3: Conjecture 7.1 follows in one line from results
   published in 2017/2018 — i.e., **before the conjecture was posed in April
   2020** — yet neither the source paper (through its Nov 2025 v2 and the
   2025 Discrete Mathematics version) nor any paper I could find states this
   resolution. I found no published resolution of Conjecture 7.1; the
   2025 WADS chordal-bipartite paper does not address it. (The catalog's
   auto-review note that "comparability graphs of S_t-free posets are not of
   bounded LMIM-width" concerns the *complement* class and is consistent with
   all of the above.)

**references_ok = true** (nothing invoked is misquoted; the failure is the
novelty claim, not a citation error).

## Caveats

1. **Novelty (the decisive issue).** Lemma 1 is Proposition 3.6 of Kang et
   al. (TCS 2017), with the same bound and proof; Lemma 2 is a re-derivation
   of standard d-neighbor-equivalence DP technology (Bui-Xuan–Telle–Vatshelle
   / Belmonte–Vatshelle), and Lemmas 2–4 together are subsumed by Golovach et
   al. (Algorithmica 2018). The writeup itself flags this honestly
   ("novelty and overlap with existing mim-width metatheorems were not
   externally checked", would_publish: false), but its claim that Lemma 1 is
   "the main new observation" is factually wrong.
2. **t = 1** (S_1-free = chains, edgeless incomparability graph) is not
   discussed; trivial, single minimal dominating set semantics still fine
   (every vertex must dominate itself; the unique minimal dominating set is V
   for the edgeless graph). Kang et al. state their result for t ≥ 2 only;
   the writeup's Lemma 1 proof itself is valid for all t ≥ 1.
3. **Input representation.** The conjecture does not fix whether the poset or
   only the graph is given; the writeup covers both (Section 5), and the
   orientation-independence argument is correct (verified). This exceeds what
   is strictly needed.
4. **Empty graph / n = 0,** disconnected graphs: nothing breaks; the DP and
   flashlight arguments are insensitive to connectivity (checked for random
   disconnected instances as part of the random-graph tests).
5. The DP exposition has minor terseness (the one-sentence converse in the
   main-states argument; U_0 being a singleton at the base case), but nothing
   that is not routine to fill, and the implementation-as-written agrees with
   brute force.
6. The verdict block's one-line claim matches what is proven (no gap between
   announcement and proof); the delay claim n^{O(t)} is what the argument
   gives.

## Referee summary

I set out to find the error and found none: every lemma re-derives correctly,
and independent implementations confirm Lemma 1 exhaustively on all ≤5-element
posets and randomly up to 11 elements, the extension-oracle DP on 480 random
instances, and the end-to-end enumerator on 133 random posets, all with zero
discrepancies. The interpretation matches the source paper's Conjecture 7.1
exactly (same S_t, same class, stronger polynomial-delay conclusion). What
fails is the novelty: the crux, Lemma 1, is verbatim Proposition 3.6 of Kang,
Kwon, Strømme and Telle (TCS 2017) — incomparability graphs of S_t-free
posets, i.e. (K_t ⊟ K_t)-free co-comparability graphs, have linear mim-width
at most t−1, with a layout computable in linear time from a co-comparability
ordering — and combining it with Golovach et al. (Algorithmica 2018,
poly-delay poly-space Dom-Enum on bounded linear mim-width) resolves
Conjecture 7.1 in one line, using only results published two years before the
conjecture appeared. Strikingly, the source paper's authors still list the
conjecture as open in their November 2025 revision, so this resolution —
correct as it is — appears to be an unnoticed corollary of the existing
literature rather than new mathematics; hence ALREADY_KNOWN rather than
CONFIRMED, with the explicit caveat that no paper states the corollary
explicitly.
