## Interpretation

The catalog statement given in `prompt.md` is:

> Let $h\colon E(K_{n,n})\to\mathbb{R}^{k}$ such that $\|h(e)\|_{1}\leq 1$ for all
> $e\in E(K_{n,n})$. Then there is a perfect matching $M$ of $K_{n,n}$ satisfying
> $f_{h}(M)=O(\sqrt{k})$.

with $f_h$ undefined. The writeup guesses $f_h(M)=\|\sum_{e\in M}h(e)-\tfrac1n\sum_{e\in E(K_{n,n})}h(e)\|_1$
and flags this as its main caveat. I fetched the source paper and checked both arXiv versions:

* **The guessed definition is exactly the paper's definition.** Page 2 of arXiv:2604.09449
  (both versions) defines $f_h(G') = \|h(G') - \tfrac{e(G')}{e(G)}h(G)\|_1$ where
  $h(G')=\sum_{e\in E(G')}h(e)$. For a perfect matching $M$ of $K_{n,n}$, $e(M)/e(K_{n,n}) = n/n^2 = 1/n$,
  which is the writeup's equation (1) verbatim (unnormalised, centred, $\ell_1$).

* **The extracted statement is v1's Conjecture 6.1, verbatim.** In **v1** (posted 2026-04-10,
  page 26): "*Let $h\colon E(K_{n,n})\to\mathbb R^k$ such that $\|h(e)\|_1\le 1$ for all
  $e\in E(K_{n,n})$. Then there is a perfect matching $M$ of $K_{n,n}$ satisfying
  $f_h(M)=O(\sqrt k)$.*" This matches the catalog extraction (the catalog auto-review is dated
  2026-05-14, i.e. it read v1).

* **v2 replaced the conjecture.** In **v2** (posted 2026-07-11, page 27), Conjecture 6.1 reads:
  "*For all positive integers $k$ and $t$, every **colour-balanced $k$-edge-coloured** $K_{kt,kt}$
  admits a perfect matching $M$ satisfying $f_c(M)=O(\sqrt k)$.*" — i.e. it is restricted from
  arbitrary $\ell_1$-bounded vector labels to actual colour-balanced colourings (labels are
  standard basis vectors with balanced colour classes). Moreover v2's acknowledgements add:
  "*Thanks also to Václav Rozhoň for pointing out an error in an earlier version of this paper*"
  (absent in v1). The v1 vector-form conjecture was therefore recognised as erroneous by the
  authors themselves and withdrawn roughly two months before today.

So: the writeup's interpretation of $f_h$ is exactly right, and its counterexample genuinely
refutes the statement it was handed. But the statement it was handed is the superseded v1 form.
Would a reasonable author of the source paper consider their conjecture resolved by this writeup?
**No.** The conjecture they now maintain (v2's Conjecture 6.1, colour-balanced colourings, upper
bound $O(k^2)$ from Theorem 1.9, lower bound $\sqrt{k/2}$ from Theorem 5.1 — the very gap the
catalog "Context" paragraph describes) is not touched by the writeup: none of its three
constructions is a colouring (construction A uses signed labels $\pm e_i$; B uses the zero
vector as a label; C uses non-basis vectors), so $f_c$ never enters. The colour-balanced
$\Theta(\sqrt k)$ question remains open. I therefore set `interpretation_ok: false` — through no
fault of the writeup's guessing (which was exactly right), the extracted statement is not the
intended, currently-open conjecture; the fault lies in the catalog's stale v1 extraction.

## Caveats

1. **Version mismatch (decisive).** The catalog entry was extracted from v1 (auto-review dated
   2026-05-14); v2 (2026-07-11) restates Conjecture 6.1 in the colour-balanced form and credits
   Rozhoň for an error in an earlier version. The attack (and the catalog's "open with high
   confidence" status) both target a statement the authors no longer maintain.
2. **The intended open problem is unresolved.** The v2 conjecture ($f_c(M)=O(\sqrt k)$ for
   colour-balanced colourings of $K_{kt,kt}$) is untouched: the writeup's labels are not
   colourings (signed labels in A, zero labels/unbalanced classes in B, non-basis vectors in C).
3. **Norm dependence.** The refutation is specific to the $\ell_1$ discrepancy; the same
   construction has $\ell_2$-discrepancy $\sqrt k$ (consistent with $O(\sqrt k)$) and
   $\ell_\infty$-discrepancy $1$. The writeup states this honestly.
4. **Parameter regime.** The counterexample needs $n=k$ (or $n\equiv$ small relative to $k$ after
   padding — padding with zero labels extends it to a lower bound of order $\min(n,k)$ for
   $n \ge k$ as well, though the writeup does not say so; as written it only needs the
   conjecture's universal quantification over $n$). For $n\ll k$ the trivial bound
   $f_h(M)\le 2n$ makes the $\ell_1$ question interesting only when $n\gtrsim\sqrt k$.
5. **$k$ even** is assumed in all three constructions; odd $k$ would need a routine adjustment
   (irrelevant to refuting a universally quantified statement).
6. The writeup's own confidence ("medium") and caveat block are appropriately honest about the
   missing definition of $f_h$; its guess turned out to be exactly the paper's definition.
