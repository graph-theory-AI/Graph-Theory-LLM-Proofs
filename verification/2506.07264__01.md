## Interpretation

The writeup proves exactly Conjecture 9.1 of arXiv:2506.07264 (Akbari, Kumar, Mohar,
Pragada, Zhang). I extracted the conjecture verbatim from the source PDF (Section 9,
"Open problems", p. 22):

> **Conjecture 9.1.** If G is a unicyclic graph of order n whose cycle has odd length
> k, then (i) s+(G) > n > s−(G), if k ≡ 3 mod 4. (ii) s+(G) < n < s−(G), if
> k ≡ 1 mod 4.

This matches the catalog statement and the writeup's theorem word for word. The
writeup adopts the standard convention that "unicyclic" means connected with exactly
one cycle; this is clearly the intended reading (the source paper motivates the
conjecture by its Proposition 9.1 on cycles C_n and treats unicyclic graphs as the
hard case of a conjecture about *connected* graphs). The writeup is transparent about
this convention and even correctly notes that a disconnected reading would make the
statement false (C_3 ∪ K_1 has s+ = 4 = n; I verified this numerically — eigenvalues
2, 0, −1, −1, so s+ = 4 exactly, breaking strictness). No interpretation gaming.

## Reference check

  above. The paper's Proposition 9.1 (explicit s±(C_n) for odd n:
  s⁺ = n − 1 + sec(π/n) for n ≡ 3, s⁺ = n + 1 − sec(π/n) for n ≡ 1 (mod 4)) is
  consistent with the writeup's theorem; I re-verified these closed forms numerically

- **Identity (1)** — invoked with a self-contained proof; it is also the standard
  Godsil–Gutman/Sachs relation φ(G) = μ(G) − 2Σ_C μ(G − V(C)) specialized to one
  cycle. Independently verified exactly by computer (see below).
- **No other external results are cited.** The proof is self-contained.
- **Priority check (the decisive finding):** arXiv:2605.24668, *"A Proof of a
  Conjecture on Positive and Negative Square Energies of Unicyclic Graphs"*, Bo Ning
  and Jing Zeng, submitted **May 23, 2026**, proves exactly this Conjecture 9.1.
  Moreover, its method is essentially identical to the writeup's: its Lemma 2.3 is
  φ_G(x) = μ_G(x) − 2μ_F(x) with F = G − V(C); its Lemma 2.4 is a Coulson-type
  integral s⁺(G) − s⁻(G) = −(4/π)∫₀^∞ t·Θ_G(t) dt; and its key claim evaluates
  i^{−n}φ_G(it) to get Θ_G(t) = ±arctan(2M_F(t)/M_G(t)) with sign depending on
  k mod 4 — step for step the writeup's argument. The catalog's "open" status was
  auto-reviewed 2026-05-14, nine days before this paper appeared, which explains the
  stale status. Given the identity of method, the writeup is plausibly a
  reproduction (from training data) of Ning–Zeng rather than an independent
  discovery; either way the result and the proof are already in the literature.

## Caveats

- **Connectivity.** The theorem needs "unicyclic" = connected (used only in step 12,
  |E| = n). The writeup states this convention explicitly and exhibits the correct
  counterexample (C_3 ∪ K_1, s⁺ = n) for the disconnected reading. This matches the
  source paper's evident intent; no issue.
- **Edge cases.** k ≥ 3 is automatic (shortest cycle); the decay argument uses
  k ≥ 3 and is stated. F = ∅ (G = C_k) works with M_F ≡ 1 and is covered by the
  computations. Zero eigenvalues are handled correctly in both s⁺+s⁻ = Σλ² and the
  sgn(λ)λ² integral representation.
- **Priority.** The writeup itself flags "publication priority was not independently
  checked" — and indeed priority fails: Ning & Zeng (arXiv:2605.24668, May 23, 2026)
  published the same theorem with the same proof strategy three months before this
  attack. The `would_publish: true` self-assessment is therefore wrong.
- No other unstated assumptions found; the proof is otherwise airtight.
