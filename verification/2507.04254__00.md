# Referee report: 2507.04254__00 (Conjecture 13, modular chromatic index of 0_k-graphs)

Target: `attacks/2507.04254__00/output.md`, which claims to **disprove** Conjecture 13 of
arXiv:2507.04254 (Berthe, Bonamy, Botler, Carenini, Colucci, Dumas, Ghasemi, Viana Neto,
*On Modular Edge Colourings of Graphs*): "Let $k$ be a positive integer. For every
$0_k$-graph $G$ we have $\chi'_k(G)\le k+o(k)$."

## Interpretation

The writeup's formalization is the intended one.

- **$0_k$-graph.** The source paper (v2, HTML) defines: "Given non-negative integers
  $\ell$ and $k$, with $\ell<k$, we say that a graph $G$ is an $\ell_k$-graph if every
  vertex of $G$ has degree $\ell$ modulo $k$." For $\ell=0$ this is exactly "every degree
  divisible by $k$". The writeup uses "all nonzero degrees divisible by $k$", which is the
  same condition (degree 0 is $\equiv 0 \bmod k$); the constructed graph has no isolated
  vertices anyway, so no discrepancy can arise. The writeup's closing caveat ("if the
  source paper imposes an additional restriction on the term $0_k$-graph...") is
  discharged: it does not.
- **$\chi'_k$.** The paper's abstract defines $\chi'_k(G)$ as the minimum number of
  colours in an edge-colouring such that in each colour class all non-zero degrees are
  $\equiv 1 \pmod k$. This is exactly what the writeup uses.
- **Conjecture 13.** Confirmed verbatim from the paper's concluding remarks: "Let $k$ be a
  positive integer. For every $0_k$-graph $G$ we have $\chi'_k(G)\leq k+o(k)$." The only
  sensible reading of $o(k)$ is a function $f(k)=o(k)$ uniform over all $0_k$-graphs $G$
  (this is also how the published refutation arXiv:2608.10687 states it, as "There exists
  a function $f(k)=o(k)$ such that every $0_k$-graph $G$ satisfies
  $\chi'_k(G)\le k+f(k)$"). The writeup adopts this reading. An infinite family
  $\{G_k\}_{k \text{ even}}$ with $\chi'_k(G_k)\ge 7k/6$ forces $f(k)\ge k/6$ on even
  $k$, so no $o(k)$ function works; restriction to even $k$ is immaterial. This is a fair
  refutation of the intended conjecture, not a strawman reading; the original authors
  would consider their conjecture resolved (indeed, essentially the same refutation has
  since been published by others and the writeup's construction has the same shape —
  bipartite $0_k$-graphs with degree set $\{k,2k\}$ — as the published one).

| 4 | $x_c\equiv y_c \pmod k$ | VALID | Bipartiteness gives $e_c=\sum_{v\in X}d_c(v)\equiv x_c$ and $e_c=\sum_{v\in Y}d_c(v)\equiv y_c \pmod k$, since each vertex incident with colour $c$ contributes $\equiv 1$. |

## Reference check

- **arXiv:2507.04254 v2** (fetched: abstract page and full HTML). Definition of
  $\ell_k$-graph, definition of $\chi'_k$, and the verbatim statement of Conjecture 13 all
  confirmed as quoted above. Theorem 2 ($\chi'_k\le 9k+f(k)$, $f\in o(k)$; $7k+f(k)$ for
  odd $k$) guarantees $\chi'_k(G_k)$ is finite, so the lower bound is about a
  well-defined quantity. The paper contains no lower bound $>k$ for $0_k$-graphs (its
  only lower-bound example is $\chi'_k(K_{1,k,k})=k+2$, not a $0_k$-graph), so the
  writeup does not contradict anything in the source paper.
- The writeup invokes **no external theorems**; the lemma and construction are
  self-contained. Nothing to flag.
- **Prior art (decisive for the verdict).** Web search found two 2026 papers that
  postdate the catalog's May 2026 auto-review and predate this report:
  - **arXiv:2608.02239** — Liu, Xu, Yang, *Linear Lower Bounds for the Modular Chromatic
    Index* (submitted 2026-08-03): bipartite graphs with
    $\chi'_k\ge k+\lfloor (k+1)/3\rfloor$ for all $k\ge2$ and
    $\chi'_k\ge 3k/2-10(k\log k)^{1/3}$ for large $k$, refuting the
    Botler–Colucci–Kohayakawa conjecture $\chi'_k\le k+C$ (which Conjecture 13 was a step
    toward).
  - **arXiv:2608.10687** — Guo, Wu, *Counterexamples to two conjectures on modular edge
    colorings of graphs* (submitted 2026-08-11): **explicitly disproves Conjecture 13**
    (cited there as Conjecture 1.2 of Berthe et al.), via connected bipartite and
    nonbipartite $0_k$-graphs with degree set $\{k,2k\}$ satisfying
    $\chi'_k(G_k)\ge(4-2\sqrt2+o(1))k\approx 1.172k$ — the same construction shape and a
    slightly stronger constant than the writeup's $7/6\approx1.167$.

  So the writeup's headline claim ("Conjecture 13 is false") was already established in
  the literature roughly three weeks before this review.

## Computational check

Scripts in `verification/scripts/2507.04254__00/`
(pure Python 3, no external deps):

1. **`construction.py`** — builds $G_k$ for $k\in\{2,4,6,8,10,12,20,50\}$ (and with a
   second choice of $S$ for $k=4,6$) and verifies: simple, bipartite, no isolated
   vertices, degree multiset exactly $\{k$ (on $X$ and $L$), $2k$ (on $H)\}$, hence a
   $0_k$-graph; $\lvert X\rvert=2k$, $\lvert Y\rvert=3k/2$, $n=7k/2$, $\rho=k/2$,
   $\lvert E\rvert=2k^2$; and re-derives the bound $\lceil nk/(n-\rho)\rceil=\lceil 7k/6\rceil$.
   **All checks passed.**
2. **`k2_exhaustive.py`** — $k=2$ (7 vertices, 8 edges): exhaustive over all $1$-, $2$-
   and $3$-colourings. Result: **0** valid colourings with $m=1$ or $m=2$; **144** valid
   with $m=3$. So $\chi'_2(G_2)=3=\lceil 7\cdot2/6\rceil$ exactly, matching the lemma.
   Additionally, in all 144 valid 3-colourings the lemma's internal identities were
   verified: $t(v)=k$ for every vertex, $x_c\equiv y_c \pmod k$,
   $u_c-v_c\equiv\lvert X\rvert-\lvert Y\rvert\pmod k$, and
   $\sum_c(u_c+v_c)=n(m-k)$.
3. **`k4_search.py`** — $k=4$ (14 vertices, 32 edges): exact backtracking solver (generic
   per-vertex feasibility pruning, no use of the lemma's reasoning). Result: $m=4$ —
   **no valid colouring** (92,594 search nodes, exact); $m=5$ — colouring found. So
   $\chi'_4(G_4)=5=\lceil 7\cdot4/6\rceil$ exactly, matching the lemma.
4. **`k6_profiles.py`** — $k=6$ (21 vertices, 72 edges; direct search infeasible): exact
   enumeration shows a degree-6 vertex has **no** admissible colour-degree profile with
   $\le5$ parts, and with $m=6$ the only profiles are $(1^6)$ for degree $6$ and
   $(7,1^5)$ for degree $12$; then every colour class has exactly $12$ edges counted from
   the $X$ side but only $\{9,15,21,27\}$ are attainable from the $Y$ side — contradiction.
   Hence $\chi'_6(G_6)\ge 7=\lceil 7\cdot6/6\rceil$, independently confirming the lemma.

No computational check failed; every claimed property of the finite construction was
reproduced with the exact numbers above.

## Caveats

- **Even $k$ only.** The construction requires $k$ even ($\lvert H\rvert=k/2$,
  $\lvert S\rvert=k/2$, $\rho=k/2$). This suffices to kill the $o(k)$ statement, but the
  writeup's one-line claim correctly says "for every even $k$". (Guo–Wu's published
  refutation handles the asymptotic statement in full.)
- **$k=1$ edge case.** Conjecture 13 says "positive integer $k$", but $\chi'_k$ is defined
  in the paper for $k\ge2$; for $k=1$ every colouring works and $\chi'_1=1$. Irrelevant to
  the refutation.
- **Uniformity of $o(k)$.** The refutation hinges on reading $o(k)$ uniformly over $G$
  (a single $f(k)=o(k)$ for all $0_k$-graphs). This is the only meaningful reading — for
  a fixed finite graph $\chi'_k(G)$ is a single number — and matches how the published
  refutation states the conjecture. Flagged by the writeup itself; not a real caveat.
- **What is proven vs. announced.** The verdict block announces
  $\chi'_k(G_k)\ge\lceil 7k/6\rceil$ for even $k$; that is exactly what the proof
  establishes (and what the code confirms, with equality at $k=2,4$). No overreach.
- **Lemma hypotheses.** "No isolated vertices" is used (for $t(v)\ge1$) and holds in
  $G_k$; the $m<2k$ hypothesis is respected via the case split at $m\ge 2k$. The
  bipartition used for $\rho$ is the explicitly constructed one, so no ambiguity about
  bipartition classes arises.
- **Novelty caveat (the operative one).** The writeup's `would_publish: true` is no
  longer tenable: arXiv:2608.10687 (2026-08-11) disproves this exact conjecture with the
  same style of construction and a stronger constant, and arXiv:2608.02239 (2026-08-03)
  gives $\approx 3k/2$ bipartite lower bounds refuting the parent conjecture. The
  attack's result is correct but subsumed.

## Referee summary

I set out to break this proof and could not. The palette-counting lemma is a clean
double-counting/parity argument, verified line by line; the construction is explicit and
every claimed degree, size, and parity was independently reproduced in code; the lower
bound $\lceil 7k/6\rceil$ was confirmed exactly at $k=2$ (exhaustive), $k=4$ (exact
search), and $k=6$ (exact profile analysis), with equality at $k=2,4$. The
interpretation matches the source paper's definitions verbatim, and the refutation
targets the intended conjecture, not a strawman. However, the result is not new:
Conjecture 13 was disproved in arXiv:2608.10687 (Guo–Wu, submitted 2026-08-11) with
bipartite and nonbipartite $0_k$-graphs of degree set $\{k,2k\}$ achieving
$(4-2\sqrt2+o(1))k>7k/6$, following arXiv:2608.02239 (Liu–Xu–Yang, 2026-08-03), which
refuted the parent Botler–Colucci–Kohayakawa conjecture with bounds approaching $3k/2$.
