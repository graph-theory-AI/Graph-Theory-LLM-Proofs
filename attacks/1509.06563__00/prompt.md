Consecutive holes in bounded clique number graphs — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 The conjecture — that for all ν > 0 and k ≥ 3 there exists n such that every graph with no k-clique and chromatic number ≥ n contains ν consecutive hole lengths — remains open. The source paper (arXiv:1509.06563) proves the triangle-free case (k=3) but explicitly states no progress on the general bounded-clique-number extension. A wide web search found no subsequent paper resolving or substantially advancing the full conjecture; related progress (e.g. arXiv:1701.07217 on long odd holes in Kk-free graphs) concerns different hole properties and does not settle consecutive lengths for general k.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up resolving the bounded-clique-number generalization was found in the indexed literature. The sole internal reference (arXiv:2306.02364) is about tournament structure and is unrelated to this conjecture. The conjecture is from 2018 and remains open with high confidence.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:A hole in a graph is an induced subgraph which is a cycle of length at least four. We prove that for every positive integer k, every triangle-free graph with sufficiently large chromatic number contains holes of k consecutive lengths.
