11/3 non-tight fractional chromatic bound for {C₄,C₅}-free planar — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 partial
 high confidence
 

 Dvořák and Hu proved every planar graph without cycles of length 4 or 5 has fractional chromatic number at most 11/3, and suspected this bound is not tight. This suspicion was confirmed by Xu and Zhu (arXiv:2511.12914, 2025), who proved such graphs are (7m:2m)-DP-colorable for every positive integer m, establishing an improved upper bound of 7/2 = 3.5 < 11/3 ≈ 3.667. The exact infimum of fractional chromatic numbers for this class remains open, currently bounded between 3 + 1/12 and 7/2.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:A graph G is (a:b)-colorable if there exists an assignment of b-element subsets of {1,...,a} to vertices of G such that sets assigned to adjacent vertices are disjoint. We show that every planar graph without cycles of length 4 or 5 is (11:3)-colorable, a weakening of recently disproved Steinberg's conjecture. In particular, each such graph with n vertices has an independent set of size at least 3n/11.
