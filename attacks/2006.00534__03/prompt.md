Threshold for minimal complements in finite groups — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 Question 10 asks whether the probability that a Bernoulli-p random subset of a finite group G is a minimal complement (or maximal supplement) exhibits a (sharp) threshold as a function of p = p(|G|). The source paper itself provides evidence for threshold behavior: Theorems 1 and 3 there show that for small p the random set is a minimal complement with high probability, and for large p it is not, but the existence and sharpness of a threshold are left open. No follow-up paper resolving this question was found in a broad search of the literature through May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper addressing the threshold question was found. The property of being a minimal complement is non-monotone, which makes standard sharp-threshold machinery (e.g., Friedgut–Kalai) not directly applicable; this non-monotonicity is explicitly noted in the source paper as the main obstacle. Searches on arXiv and via Kravitz's publication list returned no paper on this specific question. Status open with high confidence.

Question. How does the probability that a random subset of a finite group $G$ with parameter $p = p(|G|)$ is a minimal complement (or maximal supplement) vary with $p$? Is there a (sharp) threshold?

Context

Although being a minimal complement is not a monotone property, Theorems 1 and 3 together show that for small $p$ a random set is with high probability a minimal complement, and for large $p$ it is with high probability not a minimal complement, suggesting threshold behavior.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Given a subset $W$ of an abelian group $G$, a subset $C$ is called an additive complement for $W$ if $W+C=G$; if, moreover, no proper subset of $C$ has this property, then we say that $C$ is a minimal complement for $W$. It is natural to ask which subsets $C$ can arise as minimal complements for some $W$. We show that in a finite abelian group $G$, every non-empty subset $C$ of size $|C| \leq 2^{2/3}|G|^{1/3}/((3e \log |G|)^{2/3}$ is a minimal complement for some $W$. As a corollary, we deduce that every finite non-empty subset of an infinite abelian group is a minimal complement. We also derive several analogous results for ``dual'' problems about maximal supplements.
