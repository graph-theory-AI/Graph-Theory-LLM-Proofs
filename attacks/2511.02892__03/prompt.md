 Problem 4.1 asks whether every diamond-free claw-free cubic graph is strongly 6-edge-colorable, equivalently whether $\chi'_s(T(G))=6$ for every cubic graph $G$. Before the November 2025 posting, Han and Cui (2023) confirmed the conjecture for truncated prisms as a partial result, while Lv, Li, and Zhang (2022) proved $\chi'_s \le 8$ and Lin and Lin (2023) established the tight bound of 7 for the broader class of claw-free subcubic graphs. No post-November 2025 paper resolving the full conjecture was found.

Question. Is it true that every diamond-free claw-free cubic graph is strongly 6-edge-colorable?
In other words, is it true that $\chi^{\prime}_{s}(T(G))=6$ for every cubic graph $G$?

Context

Lv, Li, and Zhang proved that every claw-free subcubic graph other than the triangular prism satisfies $\chi^{\prime}_{s}(G)\leq 8$. A partial solution by Han and Cui establishes strong 6-edge-colorability for truncated prisms.
