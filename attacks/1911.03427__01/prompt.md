Induced arithmetic removal over abelian groups — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 The conjecture asking for an induced arithmetic removal lemma for arbitrary complexity-1 patterns with integer coefficients over arbitrary abelian groups remains open. A 2024 paper by Gladkova (arXiv:2412.15170) extends the Fox–Tidor–Zhao result by achieving complete removal for partition-regular complexity-1 patterns, but stays within the finite-field setting and does not address the arbitrary abelian group case. No work resolving the conjecture over general abelian groups was found in the indexed literature.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We prove an arithmetic analog of the induced graph removal lemma for complexity 1 patterns over finite fields. Informally speaking, we show that given a fixed collection of $r$-colored complexity 1 arithmetic patterns over $\mathbb F_q$, every coloring $\phi \colon \mathbb F_q^n \setminus\{0\} \to [r]$ with $o(1)$ density of every such pattern can be recolored on an $o(1)$-fraction of the space so that no such pattern remains.
