Title: Question 4.1
For which fixed digraphs $H$ does there exist a constant $c_{\varepsilon}\geq 1$ for all $\varepsilon>0$ such that every digraph $D$ without loops or parallel arcs with $\chi(D)\geq c_{\varepsilon}$ and minimum out-degree at least $\varepsilon|V(D)|$ contains $H$ as a subdigraph?

 Question 4.1 from arXiv:2503.20045 asks for which fixed digraphs H there exists a constant c_ε ≥ 1 for all ε > 0 such that every digraph D with χ(D) ≥ c_ε and minimum out-degree at least ε|V(D)| contains H as a subdigraph; this generalises the paper's main result (Theorem 1.3), which settles the question for all cycle orientations. The paper itself notes two families with affirmative answers: the cycle orientations characterised by Theorem 1.3, and subdigraphs of directed complete bipartite digraphs. No follow-up paper addressing the general question was found.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We characterize all orientations of cycles $C$ for which for every fixed $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists a constant $c \geq 1$ such that every digraph $D$ without loops or parallel arcs with $\chi(D) \geq c$ and minimum out-degree at least $\varepsilon |V(D)|$ contains $C$ as a subdigraph. This generalizes a result of Thomassen.
