f₂(d) asymptotic bound in hypercube partitions — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 The conjecture that $f_2(d)$, the number of partitions of the vertex set of $Q_d$ into vertex-disjoint 2-dimensional subcubes, obeys the same asymptotic bound as given in Proposition 1.7 with $r=2$ (namely $N^{1\pm o(1)}$) appears to remain open. The source paper was published in the Illinois Journal of Mathematics in April 2025. A related paper (arXiv:2411.04479, November 2024) studies partition counts of $\mathbb{Z}_q^n$ into large subcubes of a fixed codimension, but addresses a different setting and does not resolve the $f_2(d)$ conjecture. No follow-up work settling this conjecture was found in a wide web search.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper found that settles the conjecture. The paper appeared in Illinois Journal of Mathematics 69(1), 2025. The related paper arXiv:2411.04479 ('On the number of partitions of the hypercube Z_q^n into large subcubes', November 2024) has a main result of the form n^((q^m-1)/(q-1)) for a different partition problem and does not appear to directly address f_2(d). Conjecture is recent (2024) and high-confidence open.

 https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.00299

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Denote by Q_d the d-dimensional hypercube. Addressing a recent question we estimate the number of ways the vertex set of Q_d can be partitioned into vertex disjoint smaller cubes. Among other results, we prove that the asymptotic order of this function is not much larger than the number of perfect matchings of Q_d. We also describe several new (and old) questions.
