Title: Conjecture 1.6
For all $s \geq 1$ there exists an integer $k$ such that if $M$ is a simple binary matroid with no induced $I_s$-restriction or $PG(1, 2)$-restriction, then $\chi(M) \leq k$.

Context:
After showing that the full matroidal Gyárfás-Sumner analogue fails (Theorem 1.3), the authors conjecture that fixing $t = 2$—that is, excluding a triangle $PG(1,2)$ rather than an arbitrary projective geometry—restores a bound on the critical number. The conjecture is verified for $s \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ by Corollary 5.2, which gives $k = 1$; new ideas are needed for larger $s$.

 The conjecture is verified for $s \in \{1,2,3\}$ with $k=1$ in the source paper (Corollary 5.2). Nelson and Nomoto (arXiv:2005.00089, SIAM JDM 2022) subsequently proved the case $s=4$, showing $I_4$-free and triangle-free binary matroids have critical number at most 2. The full conjecture for all $s \geq 5$ remains open.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:An 'induced restriction' of a simple binary matroid $M$ is a restriction $M|F$, where $F$ is a flat of $M$. We consider the class $\mathcal{M}$ of all simple binary matroids $M$ containing neither a free matroid on three elements (which we call a 'claw'), nor a Fano plane as an induced restriction. We give an exact structure theorem for this class; two of its consequences are that the matroids in $\mathcal{M}$ have unbounded critical number, while the matroids in $\mathcal{M}$ not containing the clique $M(K_5)$ as an induced restriction have critical number at most $2$.
