 Conjecture 4 of arXiv:2407.18800 — that every graph drawn on the torus with edge-width at least four is 5-choosable — remains open as of May 2026. The conjecture is proposed as an important intermediate step toward the full 5-choosability characterization of toroidal graphs (Conjecture 3 of the same paper). Postle's prior result that the edge-width of a graph critical for 5-list coloring is at most logarithmic in its genus confirms the conjecture when 4 is replaced by a sufficiently large constant, but the case of edge-width exactly four is unresolved. No follow-up paper settling the conjecture was found in a web search.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper resolving Conjecture 4 was found. The conjecture is recent (July 2024). Postle's logarithmic edge-width bound for 5-list-critical graphs (cited in the source paper) already implies the conjecture for sufficiently large edge-width constants, but edge-width 4 remains open. The source paper itself makes partial progress by characterizing 5-list-coloring criticality under an additional cyclic-triangle structure condition.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Through computer-assisted enumeration, we list minimal obstructions for 5-choosability of graphs on the torus with the following additional property: There exists a cyclic system of non-contractible triangles around the torus where the consecutive triangles are at distance at most four. This condition is satisfied by all previously known obstructions, and we verify that there are no additional obstructions with this property. This supports the conjecture that a toroidal graph is 5-choosable if and only if it is 5-colorable.
