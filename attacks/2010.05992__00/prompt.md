Title: Near-sunflower analogue of the Erdős-Rado Sunflower Conjecture
If $\mathcal{F}$ is a $k$-uniform family of sets containing no near-sunflower of size $r$, then $|\mathcal{F}| \leq C^k$, where $C$ is a constant depending only on $r$.

Context:
The paper introduces near-sunflowers as a weakening of sunflowers (allowing elements to belong to $0, 1, r-1,$ or $r$ sets in the family) and proposes this bound as a weaker version of the notorious Erdős-Rado sunflower conjecture. While the paper resolves the Erdős-Szemerédi-type question (exponential bound in $n$) for near-sunflowers via Theorem 1.3, the Erdős-Rado-type question (exponential bound in $k$) is explicitly left open; Section 6 proves the bound only under a stronger condition.

=== Catalog page (statement + literature review) ===
Near-sunflower exponential bound for k-uniform families — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 The conjecture that every k-uniform family containing no near-sunflower of size r satisfies |F| ≤ C^k (C depending only on r) remains open. The source paper, published in Israel Journal of Mathematics (2023), proves the C^k bound only under the strictly stronger condition of being focal-free (Section 6), leaving the near-sunflower version explicitly open. A 2024 follow-up (Huang–Shangguan–Zhang–Zhao, arXiv:2410.23611) provides asymptotically optimal bounds for focal-free hypergraphs and codes, extending the Alon-Holzman focal-free analysis, but does not address the near-sunflower conjecture. No verified proof or counterexample for the full conjecture has been found.

 Cited literature (1)

 
 
 
partial Focal-free uniform hypergraphs and codes
 (2024)
 

 
 Xinqi Huang, Chong Shangguan, Xiande Zhang, Yuhao Zhao · arXiv preprint · arXiv:2410.23611

Gives asymptotically optimal bounds on the maximum size of focal-free uniform hypergraphs and codes (a strictly stronger condition than near-sunflower-free, as every near-sunflower-free family is also focal-free), and connects focal-free structures to the Erdős Matching Conjecture, but does not resolve the near-sunflower conjecture.
 

 

 Reviewer notes. The Erdős-Rado sunflower conjecture itself remains open; arXiv:2212.13609 (Fukuyama, 2022) claiming a proof was found to contain errors and was not accepted. Since the near-sunflower conjecture is weaker (near-sunflower-free is a stronger condition than sunflower-free), it remains open independently. The source paper was published as: Israel Journal of Mathematics, 2023. No internal references were supplied for verification.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We present some problems and results about variants of sunflowers in families of sets. In particular, we improve an upper bound of the first author, Körner and Monti on the maximum number of binary vectors of length $n$ so that every four of them are split into two pairs by some coordinate. We also propose a weaker version of the Erdős-Rado sunflower conjecture.
