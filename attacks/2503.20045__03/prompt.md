 Question 4.2 defines the directed chromatic threshold ε_H for a fixed digraph H as the minimum ε ≥ 0 such that digraphs with sufficiently high chromatic number and minimum out-degree at least ε|V(D)| must contain H as a subdigraph. The source paper itself settles the ε_H = 0 case for orientations of cycles (Theorem 1.3); the general question for arbitrary digraphs H remains open. A September 2025 follow-up (arXiv:2509.07760) studies the closely related notion of chromatic profiles δ_χ^+(H, k) for oriented cycles, proving exact thresholds for transitive tournaments and specific oriented cycles, and explicitly cites the source paper—but does not resolve Question 4.2 in general.

Question. For a fixed digraph $H$, what is the minimum value $\varepsilon_{H}\geq 0$ such that there exists a constant $c\geq 1$ such that every digraph $D$ without loops or parallel arcs with $\chi(D)\geq c$ and minimum out-degree at least $\varepsilon_{H}|V(D)|$ contains $H$ as a subdigraph?

Context

The quantity $\varepsilon_H$ provides a directed analogue of the chromatic threshold studied for undirected graphs. Theorem 1.3 may be interpreted as characterizing the orientations of cycles for which the directed chromatic threshold $\varepsilon_H$ equals zero. The authors also suggest studying variants where high chromatic number is replaced by high dichromatic number.

Notes. The authors note that $\varepsilon_H = 1$ is always an upper bound, so the quantity is well-defined for all digraphs $H$.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We characterize all orientations of cycles $C$ for which for every fixed $\varepsilon > 0$ there exists a constant $c \geq 1$ such that every digraph $D$ without loops or parallel arcs with $\chi(D) \geq c$ and minimum out-degree at least $\varepsilon |V(D)|$ contains $C$ as a subdigraph. This generalizes a result of Thomassen.
