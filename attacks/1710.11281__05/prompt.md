Cops and Robbers on constant-curvature surfaces — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status

 high confidence
 

 Mohar's question asks whether constant-curvature surfaces (spherical, Euclidean, hyperbolic) admit a simpler Cops and Robber game than general Riemannian surfaces. The 2024 paper arXiv:2402.05753 by Iršič, Mohar, and Wesolek gives a strong affirmative answer for the hyperbolic case: two cops suffice on any hyperbolic surface regardless of genus. By contrast, the cop number of general compact geodesic surfaces grows as O(g) with genus (arXiv:2205.11633). Constant negative curvature is thus confirmed to impose a dramatic simplification; the spherical and Euclidean cases remain less explicitly treated in the literature.

 Reviewer notes. The question is answered affirmatively for hyperbolic (constant negative curvature) surfaces by arXiv:2402.05753, co-authored by Mohar himself.
