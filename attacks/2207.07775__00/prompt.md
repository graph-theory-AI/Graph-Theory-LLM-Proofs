Polite integers in Ramsey multiplicity — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 Conjecture 1.5 of Fox–Wigderson states that every sufficiently large integer k is 'polite', meaning it satisfies two quantitative conditions on off-diagonal Ramsey numbers: r(k, ⌈k/2⌉) ≤ 2^{-31} r(k,k) and (r(k,k)−1)/r(k,k−1) ≥ 1 + 25(r(k,⌈k/2⌉)/r(k,k))^{1/4}. As the paper notes, the conjecture would follow from the (unproven) expectation that r(k, ⌈k/2⌉) grows exponentially slower than r(k,k) combined with a Burr–Erdős-type growth gap between consecutive diagonal Ramsey numbers. No follow-up paper resolving or making substantial progress on Conjecture 1.5 was found in the indexed literature.

 Reviewer notes. The term 'polite' is defined in arXiv:2207.07775 via two conditions on off-diagonal Ramsey numbers r(k, ⌈k/2⌉) vs r(k,k) and r(k,k-1). The conjecture is intimately tied to two longstanding open problems in Ramsey theory (the gap between diagonal and off-diagonal Ramsey numbers, and the consecutive diagonal Ramsey growth rate), making it unlikely to be resolved without progress on those foundational questions. The related follow-up arXiv:2309.06959 ('Turán Colourings in Off-Diagonal Ramsey Multiplicity') extends some results from the paper but does not address Conjecture 1.5. No follow-up found after exhausting 5 web calls.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Extending an earlier conjecture of Erdős, Burr and Rosta conjectured that among all two-colorings of the edges of a complete graph, the uniformly random coloring asymptotically minimizes the number of monochromatic copies of any fixed graph $H$. This conjecture was disproved independently by Sidorenko and Thomason. The first author later found quantitatively stronger counterexamples, using the Turán coloring, in which one of the two colors spans a balanced complete multipartite graph.
We prove that the Turán coloring is extremal for an infinite family of graphs, and that it is the unique extremal coloring. This yields the first determination of the Ramsey multiplicity constant of a graph for which the Burr--Rosta conjecture fails.
We also prove an analogous three-color result. In this case, our result is conditional on a certain natural conjecture on the behavior of two-color Ramsey numbers.
