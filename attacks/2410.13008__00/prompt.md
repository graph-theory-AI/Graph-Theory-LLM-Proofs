Title: Conjecture 1.2
Let $G$ be a strongly 2-connected digraph drawn (possibly with crossings) in an annulus $\Sigma$, such that every directed cycle has winding number one around the annulus in the natural sense. Then $G$ can be drawn in $\Sigma$ without crossings, such that every directed cycle has winding number one.

Context:
This conjecture arises from an analogy with Thomassen's theorem on acyclic digraphs: gluing the north and south sides of a rectangle produces an annulus, and the question becomes whether a drawing with crossings in the annulus can be continuously deformed to a crossing-free drawing while preserving winding numbers of directed cycles. The author states explicitly that 'we cannot prove it.'
