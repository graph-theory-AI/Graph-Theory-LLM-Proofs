 The q-color generalization of Conjecture 1.2 from Fox–Tidor–Zhang (arXiv:2312.06895, published JCTB 176, 2026, pp. 268–286) concerns the q-color analogue of the F-Ramsey number identity $r_{K_3}(K_t) = \binom{r(K_t)}{3}$ for all t. The paper proves the q-color generalizations of its three main theorems (1.1, 1.3, 1.4) by the same arguments, but leaves the q-color analogue of Conjecture 1.2 explicitly open. No follow-up paper resolving this conjecture was found in a wide literature search as of May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found. The paper was published in Journal of Combinatorial Theory Series B 176 (2026) pp. 268–286. Conjecture 1.2 (two-color version) concerns the formula $r_{K_3}(K_t) = \binom{r(K_t)}{3}$ for all t (not just sufficiently large t); the q-color generalization conjectures the analogous identity for q-color F-Ramsey numbers $r_{K_3}^{(q)}(K_t)$. The q-color versions of the proved theorems follow by the same proofs; only the conjecture (requiring a different argument for all t) remains open.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:A graph is $H$-Ramsey if every two-coloring of its edges contains a monochromatic copy of $H$. Define the $F$-Ramsey number of $H$, denoted by $r_F(H)$, to be the minimum number of copies of $F$ in a graph which is $H$-Ramsey. This generalizes the Ramsey number and size Ramsey number of a graph. Addressing a question of Spiro, we prove that \[r_{K_3}(K_t)=\binom{r(K_t)}{3}\] for all sufficiently large $t$. We do so through a result on graph coloring: there exists an absolute constant $K$ such that every $r$-chromatic graph where every edge is contained in at least $K$ triangles must contain at least $\binom{r}{3}$ triangles in total.
