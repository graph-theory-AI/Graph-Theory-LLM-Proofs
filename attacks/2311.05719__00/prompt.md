 Conjecture 1.3 from arXiv:2311.05719 proposes that for every fixed $t \geq 1$, the family of $t$-clock-free graphs is clean. The source paper itself proves the $t=2$ case (all clocks) as its main result (Theorem 1.1). No subsequent paper resolving the conjecture for general $t$ was found after an exhaustive search of the 'Induced subgraphs and tree decompositions' series through paper XIX (2025) and a broad literature search; the conjecture remains open as of May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. The main result of arXiv:2311.05719 (Theorem 1.1) establishes cleanness for the class of all clock-free graphs (t=2 case). Conjecture 1.3 generalises this: a t-clock allows close non-adjacent pairs (distance at most t-1 along the hole), and cleanness is conjectured to persist for every fixed threshold t. The series continues through paper XIX ('Thetas and forests', 2025, arXiv:2506.05602) by Chudnovsky, Codsi, and Spirkl, but none of papers XV–XIX appear to address t-clock-free graphs. No follow-up resolving this conjecture was found in the indexed literature.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:A clock is a graph consisting of an induced cycle $C$ and a vertex not in $C$ with at least two non-adjacent neighbours in $C$. We show that every clock-free graph of large treewidth contains a "basic obstruction" of large treewidth as an induced subgraph: a complete graph, a subdivision of a wall, or the line graph of a subdivision of a wall.
