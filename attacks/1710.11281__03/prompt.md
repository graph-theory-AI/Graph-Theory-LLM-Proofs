Worst fixed-genus surface for cop number — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 medium confidence
 

 The question of which Riemannian surfaces of fixed genus maximise the cop number in the continuous Cops and Robbers game remains open. Mohar's follow-up work (arXiv:2205.11633) develops the geodesic-space framework and establishes that the cop number of genus-$g$ surfaces satisfies $c = O(g)$ and $c = \Omega(\sqrt{g})$, bounding the worst case but not identifying which geometric realisations achieve it. Ir\v{s}i\v{c}, Mohar, and Wesolek (arXiv:2402.05753) show that on surfaces of constant negative curvature (hyperbolic manifolds) two cops suffice to come arbitrarily close to the robber regardless of genus, ruling out hyperbolic surfaces as the worst-case examples.
