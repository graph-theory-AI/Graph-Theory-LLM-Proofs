Subexponential gap for dimension-2 subcube partitions — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 Problem 1.10 from arXiv:2401.00299 asks whether $f(d)/f_{\leq 2}(d)$ is subexponential in $n = 2^{d-1}$, where $f(d)$ counts all subcube partitions of the $d$-dimensional hypercube and $f_{\leq 2}(d)$ counts those restricted to subcubes of dimension at most 2. The source paper (published in the Illinois Journal of Mathematics, April 2025) poses this as an open problem addressing the gap between unrestricted and dimension-at-most-2 subcube partitions. No follow-up resolving this question has been found in the literature as of May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up resolving Problem 1.10 found after 3 targeted searches. The related paper arXiv:2411.04479 (Tarannikov, Nov 2024) addresses asymptotic counts for Z_q^n partitions into large subcubes but does not address this specific ratio. The conjecture is recent (arXiv preprint January 2024, journal publication November 2024) and absence of follow-up is consistent with an open problem.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Denote by Q_d the d-dimensional hypercube. Addressing a recent question we estimate the number of ways the vertex set of Q_d can be partitioned into vertex disjoint smaller cubes. Among other results, we prove that the asymptotic order of this function is not much larger than the number of perfect matchings of Q_d. We also describe several new (and old) questions.
