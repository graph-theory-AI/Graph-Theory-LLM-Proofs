Conjecture. Every bichromatic-forbidding 4-candidate can be obtained from the diamond by a sequence of the operations described in Lemmas 27, 28, and 29.

Context

A plane graph with outer face bounded by a 4-cycle is bichromatic-forbidding if no 4-colouring assigns the same colour to opposite pairs of the bounding 4-cycle. A 4-candidate is a specific class of such graphs arising in the structural analysis of non-4-colourable graphs in $\mathcal{C}$. The conjecture proposes a complete recursive generation scheme for this class via three explicit expansion operations.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We conjecture that every graph of minimum degree five with no separating triangles and drawn in the plane with one crossing is 4-colorable. In this paper, we use computer enumeration to show that this conjecture holds for all graphs with at most 28 vertices, explore the consequences of this conjecture and provide some insights on how it could be proved.
