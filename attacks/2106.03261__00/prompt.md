 The central question of Conlon–Fox–Sudakov–Zhao (arXiv:2106.03261) — for which graphs $F$ is there an $F$-counting lemma in $C_4$-free graphs? — remains open as of 2026. The paper establishes that girth at least 5 is a necessary condition for countability and constructs infinite families of countable graphs (including $C_5$), but a complete characterization is not known. The status of specific 3-regular graphs of girth 5 such as the Petersen graph and the dodecahedral graph is still unresolved, as noted in subsequent survey literature (e.g., Zhao's textbook slides).

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper resolving the full characterization was found across four targeted web searches. The paper is at v1 (no revision) as of the review date. Zhao's sparse regularity slides (yufeizhao.com) list the Petersen and dodecahedral graphs as explicit open problems, confirming the conjecture remains open. The 2-density condition ($m_2(F) < 2$) is a further necessary condition discussed informally in the paper.

Context

This is the central open problem of the paper. The authors formally define what it means for a graph $F$ to be 'countable' (Definition 1.3) and construct infinite families of countable graphs, but a complete characterization remains open. They note that a necessary condition is that $F$ has girth at least 5 (Remark 1.6).

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:For which graphs $F$ is there a sparse $F$-counting lemma in $C_4$-free graphs? We are interested in identifying graphs $F$ with the property that, roughly speaking, if $G$ is an $n$-vertex $C_4$-free graph with on the order of $n^{3/2}$ edges, then the density of $F$ in $G$, after a suitable normalization, is approximately at least the density of $F$ in an $\epsilon$-regular approximation of $G$. In recent work, motivated by applications in extremal and additive combinatorics, we showed that $C_5$ has this property. Here we construct a family of graphs with the property.
