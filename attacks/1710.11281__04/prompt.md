Title: Question (Riemannian surfaces): supremum of cop number for geometrically bounded surfaces
What is the supremum of the cop numbers taken over all surfaces whose cut locus is at least $1$ and whose area is at most $\alpha$? Is it bounded?

=== Catalog page (statement + literature review) ===
Cop number finiteness on bounded Riemannian surfaces — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 partial
 medium confidence
 

 No paper appears to directly resolve the question for arbitrary Riemannian surfaces with positive injectivity radius (cut locus ≥1) and bounded area. Two closely related developments have appeared since 2017: arXiv:2205.11633 establishes that the cop number of any compact geodesic surface is bounded as a function of its genus (c=3 for low genus, c=O(g) in general), and arXiv:2402.05753 shows that two cops suffice (in the sense of coming arbitrarily close to the robber) on hyperbolic surfaces of any genus. Because bounded injectivity radius together with bounded area classically forces bounded genus, the result of 2205.11633 indirectly supports an affirmative answer to the question, but this connection does not appear to be made explicit in either paper.

Proves that for every compact geodesic surface the cop number satisfies c=3 for orientable surfaces of genus ≤1 and c=O(g), c=Ω(√g) for larger genus g; combined with the classical fact that bounded injectivity radius and bounded area imply bounded genus, this indirectly suggests the supremum is finite in the setting of the question.
 

 
 
partial Cops and Robber on Hyperbolic Manifolds
 (2024)

Shows that two cops can come arbitrarily close to the robber on hyperbolic surfaces with constant curvature −1 independently of the genus, establishing strong boundedness of the cop number for the most natural class of geometrically bounded Riemannian surfaces.
