 Conjecture 1.2 of Alon–Defant (arXiv:1904.02595) asserts that α(G) = IR(G) for any direct product G of complete multipartite graphs (not necessarily balanced). The paper itself proves the weaker balanced version (Burcroff's Conjecture 1.1) in all but at most 37 exceptional cases, but leaves the general unbalanced strengthening (Conjecture 1.2) open. A search of the literature up to May 2026 found no follow-up paper resolving Conjecture 1.2; Defant's subsequent research has moved toward algebraic combinatorics, and no other authors appear to have published on this specific problem.

Conjecture. If $H_1, \ldots, H_n$ are complete multipartite graphs and $G = \prod_{i=1}^{n} H_i$, then $\alpha(G) = IR(G)$.

Context

The authors propose this as a further strengthening of Burcroff's Conjecture 1.1, removing the assumption that the complete multipartite graphs in the direct product are balanced. It is stated at the end of the introduction after the main results are summarised.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:The direct product of graphs $G_1,\ldots,G_n$ is the graph with vertex set $V(G_1)\times\cdots\times V(G_n)$ in which two vertices $(g_1,\ldots,g_n)$ and $(g_1',\ldots,g_n')$ are adjacent if and only if $g_i$ is adjacent to $g_i'$ in $G_i$ for all $i$. Building off of the recent work of Brakensiek, we prove an optimal vertex isoperimetric inequality for direct products of complete multipartite graphs. Applying this inequality, we derive a stability result for independent sets in direct products of balanced complete multipartite graphs, showing that every large independent set must be close to the maximal independent set determined by setting one of the coordinates to be constant. Armed with these isoperimetry and stability results, we prove that the upper irredundance number of a direct product of balanced complete multipartite graphs is equal to its independence number in all but at most $37$ cases. This proves most of a conjecture of Burcroff that arose as a strengthening of a conjecture of the second author and Iyer. We also propose a further strengthening of Burcroff's conjecture.
