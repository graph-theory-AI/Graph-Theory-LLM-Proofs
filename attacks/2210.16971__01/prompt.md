 Conjecture 1.7 from arXiv:2210.16971 posits that admitting a homomorphism to a directed edge and having a cycle in the underlying undirected graph are together sufficient for an oriented graph to have the directed forcing property — a directed analogue of the Skokan–Thoma forcing conjecture. The source paper (published in Electronic Journal of Combinatorics) proves these two conditions are necessary (Theorem 1.6) but the conjectured sufficiency remains open. A wide web search covering related work on directed Sidorenko and forcing properties found no follow-up paper resolving the conjecture as of May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper resolving or partially resolving Conjecture 1.7 was found. The source paper was published in the Electronic Journal of Combinatorics (v32i3p38). Related papers on tournament Sidorenko inequalities (arXiv:2402.08418, arXiv:2512.11222) were fetched and verified to not address the directed forcing conjecture.
