Conjecture. If $k>s$ and $H$ is a $k$-graph which is $s$-tightly connected and not $k$-partite, then there exists a positive constant $c$ such that $r(H,K_{n}^{(k)})\geq t_{s}(n^{c})$, where the tower function $t_{i}(x)$ is defined by $t_{1}(x)=x$ and $t_{i}(x)=2^{t_{i-1}(x)}$ for all $i\geq 2$.

 When are off-diagonal hypergraph Ramsey numbers polynomial?

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:A natural open problem in Ramsey theory is to determine those $3$-graphs $H$ for which the off-diagonal Ramsey number $r(H, K_n^{(3)})$ grows polynomially with $n$. We make substantial progress on this question by showing that if $H$ is tightly connected or has at most two tight components, then $r(H, K_n^{(3)})$ grows polynomially if and only if $H$ is contained in an iterated blowup of an edge.
