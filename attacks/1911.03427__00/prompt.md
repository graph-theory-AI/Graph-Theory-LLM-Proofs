Induced arithmetic removal for higher complexity patterns — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 The open problem—extending the induced arithmetic removal lemma to higher complexity patterns and to general abelian groups (including Z)—remains unresolved. Fox, Tidor, and Zhao established the lemma for complexity 1 patterns over finite fields in 2019; the original paper was published in Israel Journal of Mathematics (2022). A 2024 follow-up by Gladkova (arXiv:2412.15170) refines the complexity 1/finite-field result by proving that complete removal (no non-generic exceptions) is achievable for partition-regular patterns of complexity 1, but this does not address either of the two main open directions: higher complexity patterns or general abelian groups.

Problem. Extend the induced arithmetic removal lemma beyond complexity 1 patterns over finite fields to higher complexity patterns and to general abelian groups (even the case of $\mathbb{Z}$ is open).

Context

After establishing Theorem 1.14 for complexity 1 patterns over $\mathbb{F}_q$, the authors note that extending to higher complexity and to general groups remains open. They observe that for affine-invariant patterns (which avoid a regularisation difficulty near the origin), the problem for bounded complexity patterns over finite fields has been solved, but the general induced setting is still unresolved.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We prove an arithmetic analog of the induced graph removal lemma for complexity 1 patterns over finite fields. Informally speaking, we show that given a fixed collection of $r$-colored complexity 1 arithmetic patterns over $\mathbb F_q$, every coloring $\phi \colon \mathbb F_q^n \setminus\{0\} \to [r]$ with $o(1)$ density of every such pattern can be recolored on an $o(1)$-fraction of the space so that no such pattern remains.
