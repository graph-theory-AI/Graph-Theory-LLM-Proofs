 The informal problem asks whether near-extremal intersecting r-partite hypergraphs H with \tau(H) close to r must contain large pieces from a finite projective plane, or whether genuinely different constructions exist. No follow-up paper specifically resolving either direction was found. The broader literature on Ryser's conjecture confirms that truncated projective planes remain essentially the only known near-extremal constructions, consistent with the authors' observation, but no structural theorem characterising near-extremal examples has been established.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found specifically addressing this informal problem. === Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We give a construction of r-partite r-uniform intersecting hypergraphs with cover number at least r-4 for all but finitely many r. This answers a question of Abu-Khazneh, Barat, Pokrovskiy and Szabo, and shows that a long-standing unsolved conjecture due to Ryser is close to being best possible for every value of r.
