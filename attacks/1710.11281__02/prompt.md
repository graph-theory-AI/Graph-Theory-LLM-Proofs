Title: Question (Riemannian surfaces): cop converging to robber
If the cops have a strategy to catch the robber, could it in fact be achieved that one of the cops comes arbitrarily close to the robber (or even onto the same point) in the continuous version of the game?

Context:
In the continuous game on a Riemannian surface a robber is 'caught' when a cop comes within distance $\frac{1}{2}$. The author asks whether a winning strategy can always be strengthened so that a cop actually converges to the robber's exact position.

Cop convergence on Riemannian surfaces — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 partial
 medium confidence
 

 The question asks whether a winning cop strategy on a Riemannian surface (where capture means coming within distance 1/2) can always be strengthened so that a cop converges to the robber's exact position. For hyperbolic surfaces (constant negative curvature), arXiv:2402.05753 (2024) proves a positive partial answer: two cops have a strategy to come arbitrarily close to the robber, regardless of the genus. The general question for arbitrary Riemannian surfaces appears to remain open.

 Cited literature (2)

 (2022)
 

 
 unknown (ADS ID: 2022arXiv220511633M) · Canadian Journal of Mathematics · arXiv:2205.11633

Introduces the Cops and Robber game on compact geodesic spaces, proves that every compact geodesic surface has finite cop number depending only on genus, and establishes the framework in which Mohar's convergence question is most naturally formulated.
 

 
 
partial Cops and Robber on Hyperbolic Manifolds
 (2024)
 

 
 unknown · arXiv preprint · arXiv:2402.05753

Proves that on surfaces of constant negative curvature (hyperbolic surfaces), two cops have a strategy to come arbitrarily close to the robber independently of the genus, giving a positive partial answer to Mohar's convergence question for hyperbolic Riemannian surfaces.
