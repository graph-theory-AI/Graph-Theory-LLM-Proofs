Conjecture. Every subcubic triangle-free graph avoiding $F^{(1)}_{14}$, $F^{(2)}_{14}$, $F_{11}$, and $F_{22}$ as subgraphs is fractionally $19/7$-colorable.

Context

The authors pose this as an intermediate conjecture between their main result (Theorem 1.4, giving $11/4$-colorability when only $F^{(1)}_{14}$ and $F^{(2)}_{14}$ are excluded) and Conjecture 1.3 (giving $8/3$-colorability when six graphs are excluded). It is noted that $F_{11}$ and $F_{22}$ both have fractional chromatic number exactly $11/4$, while $F^{(1)}_{19}$ and $F^{(2)}_{19}$ have fractional chromatic number $19/7$, motivating this intermediate bound.

Notes. PDF source — the bound $19/7$ and the excluded subgraphs are stated clearly in the text.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We prove that up to two exceptions, every connected subcubic triangle-free graph has fractional chromatic number at most 11/4. This is tight unless further exceptional graphs are excluded, and improves the known bound on the fractional chromatic number of subcubic triangle-free planar graphs.
