Hoppers existence for large Euler genus — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 Hoppers are a special class of 3-connected building blocks for obstructions of connectivity two for surface embeddings of arbitrary Euler genus. Mohar and Škoda prove in arXiv:2002.00258 that hoppers do not exist for Euler genus 2 (the Klein bottle case), while the companion Part II (arXiv:2002.00261) handles cascades. The authors believe hoppers will appear for sufficiently large Euler genus but provide no construction, and no subsequent paper constructing or ruling out hoppers for any larger genus has been identified in the indexed literature.

 Reviewer notes. The companion paper arXiv:2002.00261 (posted the same date) classifies cascades for Euler genus 2 but does not address hoppers for larger genus. The source paper was published in J. Combinatorial Theory, Series B, Vol. 164, pp. 299–320, 2024, but this is merely the journal version of the 2020 preprint. No follow-up constructing or disproving hoppers for any Euler genus > 2 was found after four targeted web searches.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Graphs that are critical (minimal excluded minors) for embeddability in surfaces are studied. In Part I we consider the structure of graphs with a 2-vertex-cut that are critical with respect to the Euler genus. A general theorem describing the building blocks is presented. These constituents, called hoppers and cascades, are classified for the case when Euler genus is small. As a consequence, the complete list of obstructions of connectivity 2 for embedding graphs into the Klein bottle is obtained. This is the first complete result about obstructions for embeddability of graphs in the Klein bottle, and the outcome is somewhat surprising in the sense that there are considerably fewer excluded minors than expected.
