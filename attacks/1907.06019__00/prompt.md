Extremal self-annihilating subspaces in exterior algebra — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 medium confidence
 

 Scott and Wilmer prove (Theorem 2.3) that a self-annihilating subspace $W \subseteq \bigwedge^r V$ satisfies $\dim W \leq \binom{n-1}{r-1}$ for $r \leq n/2$, with the bound achieved by spaces of the form $\{v \wedge z : z \in \bigwedge^{r-1} V\}$. They verify that these are the only extremal spaces for $r=1$ and $r=2$ and ask whether this holds in general. No follow-up paper resolving the question for $r \geq 3$ was found across five web searches; the conjecture appears to remain open.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found in five web calls. The paper was published in the Journal of the London Mathematical Society (2021). A related paper arXiv:1911.07011 ('Bollobás-type theorems for hemi-bundled two families') was found but its abstract does not indicate it addresses this specific characterization question. Confidence is medium rather than high because the conjecture is roughly five years old and the niche topic may have seen progress in venues not easily indexed.

Informal. For $r < n/2$, are all extremal self-annihilating subspaces $W \subseteq \bigwedge^r V$ with $\dim W = \binom{n-1}{r-1}$ of the form $\{v \wedge z : z \in \bigwedge^{r-1} V\}$ for some fixed $v \in V$?

Context

Theorem 2.3 shows that a self-annihilating subspace $W \subseteq \bigwedge^r V$ satisfies $\dim W \leq \binom{n-1}{r-1}$ for $r \leq n/2$, with equality achieved by spaces of the form $\{v \wedge z : z \in \bigwedge^{r-1} V\}$. The authors verify this characterization for $r = 1$ and $r = 2$ and ask whether it holds in general.

Notes. Stated in running prose after Theorem 2.3: 'It is an interesting question to describe the extremal examples for Theorem 2.3 ... Could all extremal examples be of this form?' PDF source.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We investigate the combinatorial structure of subspaces of the exterior algebra of a finite-dimensional real vector space, working in parallel with the extremal combinatorics of hypergraphs. Using initial monomials, projections of the underlying vector space onto subspaces, and the interior product, we find analogues of local and global LYM inequalities, the Erdős-Ko-Rado theorem, and the Ahlswede-Khachatrian bound for $t$-intersecting hypergraphs.
Using these tools, we prove a new extension of the Two Families Theorem of Bollobás, giving a weighted bound for subspace configurations satisfying a skew cross-intersection condition. We also verify a recent conjecture of Gerbner, Keszegh, Methuku, Abhishek, Nagy, Patkós, Tompkins, and Xiao on pairs of set systems satisfying both an intersection and a cross-intersection condition.
