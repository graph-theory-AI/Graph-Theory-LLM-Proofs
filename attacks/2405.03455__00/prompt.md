Problem. Determine the correct dependence of $ES_{\ell}(n)$ on $\ell$.

Context

The paper establishes that $(3\ell-1)\cdot 2^{n-5} < ES_{\ell}(n) < \ell^{2}\cdot 2^{n+C\sqrt{n\log n}}$, leaving a gap in the dependence on $\ell$. In the regime where $n$ is fixed and $\ell\to\infty$, the cups-caps theorem gives $ES_{\ell}(n)=O(\ell)$, which is best possible up to constants, but the general joint dependence on both parameters remains open.

Notes. Stated in running prose at the end of the introduction: "It remains an interesting open problem to determine the correct dependence of $ES_{\ell}(n)$ on $\ell$."

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Let $ES_{\ell}(n)$ be the minimum $N$ such that every $N$-element point set in the plane contains either $\ell$ collinear members or $n$ points in convex position. We prove that there is a constant $C>0$ such that, for each $\ell, n \ge 3$,
$$ (3\ell - 1) \cdot 2^{n-5} < ES_{\ell}(n) < \ell^2 \cdot 2^{n+ C\sqrt{n\log n}}.$$ A similar extension of the well-known Erd\H os--Szekeres cups-caps theorem is also proved.
