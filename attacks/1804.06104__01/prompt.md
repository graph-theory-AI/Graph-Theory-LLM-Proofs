 This is an informal meta-conjecture by Joret and Lochet that probabilistic methods such as the Lovász Local Lemma or entropy compression will not suffice on their own to prove the $\Delta+2$ bound for adjacent vertex distinguishing edge colouring. As of 2026, the full $\Delta+2$ conjecture of Zhang, Liu, and Wang remains open, with the best known general upper bound still being $\Delta+19$ established in the source paper itself. No subsequent work has achieved $\Delta+2$ by any method (which would have forced a reassessment of the conjecture's plausibility), and no formal analysis has appeared confirming or refuting the insufficiency claim about probabilistic techniques.

 Reviewer notes. This conjecture is informal and not mathematically falsifiable in the usual sense — it would be 'disproved' only if someone produced a proof of the $\Delta+2$ bound using purely probabilistic methods. No such proof exists as of the review date. The status 'open' reflects that the underlying $\Delta+2$ conjecture is unresolved and no follow-up paper has addressed the probabilistic-methods limitation claim directly. The best general bound remains $\Delta+19$ (Joret–Lochet 2020, the source paper). A December 2025 arXiv paper (2512.10827) addresses vertex-distinguishing edge coloring but is a distinct problem.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:A proper edge colouring of a graph is adjacent vertex distinguishing if no two adjacent vertices see the same set of colours. Using a clever application of the Local Lemma, Hatami (2005) proved that every graph with maximum degree $\Delta$ and no isolated edge has an adjacent vertex distinguishing edge colouring with $\Delta + 300$ colours, provided $\Delta$ is large enough. We show that this bound can be reduced to $\Delta + 19$. This is motivated by the conjecture of Zhang, Liu, and Wang (2002) that $\Delta + 2$ colours are enough for $\Delta \geq 3$.
