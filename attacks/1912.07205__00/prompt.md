 Conjecture 8 from arXiv:1912.07205 (Mohar and Singer, published in European Journal of Combinatorics 2021) asserts that every planar triangulation with a disconnected 4-coloring complex must contain at least one even-parity and one odd-parity component. The conjecture was posed as an open problem based on extensive computation, after the paper resolved Tutte's original question by constructing planar triangulations with arbitrarily many same-parity components. No follow-up work resolving this conjecture was found in the indexed literature through May 2026.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:In 1999, at one of his last public lectures, Tutte discussed a question he had considered since the times of the Four Color Conjecture. He asked whether the 4-coloring complex of a planar triangulation could have two components in which all colorings had the same parity. In this note we answer Tutte's question to the contrary of his speculations by showing that there are triangulations of the plane whose coloring complexes have arbitrarily many even and odd components. We end up with a closely related conjecture, which is based on an extensive computation, and which claims that for every planar triangulation whose 4-coloring complex is disconnected has a component of even parity and one of odd parity.
