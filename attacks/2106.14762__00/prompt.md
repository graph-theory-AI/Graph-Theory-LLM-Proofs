Informal. The pointwise density of $R$ in $C^+ = \{(x,y)\in[0,1]^2 : x < ye^{1-y}\}$ depends only on the vertical coordinate $y$ (``horizontal uniformity''). The authors state they do not know how to establish this directly, that is, without recourse to the explicit characterization of $R$.

Context

After defining the runsort permuton $R$ and noting that its absolutely continuous part has support $C^+$, the authors observe that the density in $C^+$ is independent of $x$. They remark that this horizontal uniformity is not combinatorially obvious and that a direct combinatorial proof is unknown.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Suppose we choose a permutation $\pi$ uniformly at random from $S_n$. Let $\mathsf{runsort}(\pi)$ be the permutation obtained by sorting the ascending runs of $\pi$ into lexicographic order. Alexandersson and Nabawanda recently asked if the plot of $\mathsf{runsort}(\pi)$, when scaled to the unit square $[0,1]^2$, converges to a limit shape as $n\to\infty$. We answer their question by showing that the measures corresponding to the scaled plots of these permutations $\mathsf{runsort}(\pi)$ converge with probability $1$ to a permuton (limiting probability distribution) that we describe explicitly. In particular, the support of this permuton is $\{(x,y)\in[0,1]^2:x\leq ye^{1-y}\}$.
