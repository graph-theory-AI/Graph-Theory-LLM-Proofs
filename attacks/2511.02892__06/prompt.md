½-flow-pair existence in bridgeless graphs — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status

 Conjecture 6.1 from arXiv:2511.02892, introduced in the context of the workshop in November 2025, postulates the existence of a so-called 1/2-flow-pair (a 2-flow and a 4-flow with a compatibility condition on zero edges) in every bridgeless graph; such a pair would imply Tutte's 5-flow conjecture via a circular 5-flow construction generalising Seymour's 6-flow proof. No resolution or partial progress beyond the computational verification reported in the source paper (cyclically 4-edge-connected snarks up to 34 vertices, snarks with circular flow number 5 on 36 vertices, snarks of oddness 4 up to 44 vertices) was found in the literature as of May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. The foundational framework of t-flow-pairs (of which the 1/2-flow-pair is a special case) is introduced in the companion paper 'Manhattan and Chebyshev flows' by Gáborik, Kurz, Mazzuoccolo, Rajník, Rieg (arXiv:2510.22234, October 2025), which predates the workshop problem paper. No follow-up paper proving or disproving Conjecture 6.1 was found. The conjecture is very recent (~6 months old) and the absence of follow-up is expected.

Conjecture. Each bridgeless graph admits a $2$-flow $\varphi_{2}$ and a $4$-flow $\varphi_{4}$ such that for any edge $e$, if $\varphi_{2}(e)=0$, then $|\varphi_{4}(e)|\geq 2$.

Context

The conjecture implies the 5-flow conjecture since $(5\varphi_{2}+\varphi_{4})/2$ is a circular 5-flow, and generalises Seymour's approach in the proof of the 6-flow theorem. The resulting pair is called a $1/2$-flow-pair, and the conjecture has been verified computationally for all cyclically 4-edge-connected snarks up to 34 vertices.
