Quantum ergodicity failure in narrow spectral windows — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 open
 high confidence
 

 The open problem of showing that the quantum-ergodicity average in a small spectral window of width roughly 1/log(m) fails to be equidistributed on near-Ramanujan graphs remains unresolved. Theo McKenzie (2022) studied the necessity of conditions for graph quantum ergodicity and cited 1908.03694, but addressed a different question about which graph-theoretic properties individually suffice for QE. No paper was found that controls all eigenvectors in the small spectral window to prove the QE average is not equidistributed there, nor that determines a lower bound on the smallest such window.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper was found that directly addresses the open problem of proving QE fails in small spectral windows of width ~1/log(m) on near-Ramanujan graphs. Theo McKenzie (2022) is the closest related work found but focuses on necessity of conditions for QE broadly, not the small-window question. The paper arXiv:2102.04169 (Quantum ergodicity for expanding quantum graphs in the regime of spectral delocalization) concerns metric/quantum graphs rather than combinatorial graphs and addresses when QE holds, not when it fails. The conjecture is from 2019 and no resolution was found across the web search.

Problem. The problem of controlling the other eigenvectors well enough to say that the quantum-ergodicity average in a small spectral window $I \subset (-2\sqrt{d}, 2\sqrt{d})$ of width roughly $1/\log(m)$ is not equidistributed is not pursued here and remains open. It would also be interesting to determine whether the constructions of Theorem 1.3 can prove a lower bound on the length of the smallest such window that is possible.

Context

The works [ALM15, BLML15] prove quantum ergodicity on sequences of expander graphs when the spectral average (3) is taken over a window $I$ of width roughly $1/\log(m)$. Theorem 1.3 produces many localized eigenvectors concentrated in a very small spectral window due to high eigenvalue multiplicity, but whether this implies that the QE average itself fails to be equidistributed in that window requires controlling all other eigenvectors in the window, which the paper does not achieve.

 High-girth near-Ramanujan graphs with localized eigenvectors
 Noga Alon, Shirshendu Ganguly, Nikhil Srivastava · 2019-08-10
 https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.03694
 PDF source

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We show that for every prime $d$ and $\alpha\in (0,1/6)$, there is an infinite sequence of $(d+1)$-regular graphs $G=(V,E)$ with girth at least $2\alpha \log_{d}(|V|)(1-o_d(1))$, second adjacency matrix eigenvalue bounded by $(3/\sqrt{2})\sqrt{d}$, and many eigenvectors fully localized on small sets of size $O(|V|^\alpha)$. This strengthens the results of Ganguly-Srivastava, who constructed high girth (but not expanding) graphs with similar properties, and may be viewed as a discrete analogue of the "scarring" phenomenon observed in the study of quantum ergodicity on manifolds. Key ingredients in the proof are a technique of Kahale for bounding the growth rate of eigenfunctions of graphs, discovered in the context of vertex expansion and a method of Erdős and Sachs for constructing high girth regular graphs.
