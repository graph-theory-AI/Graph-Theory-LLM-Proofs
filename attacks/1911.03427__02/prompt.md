 The conjecture that $\delta = \epsilon^{O_A(1)}$ (polynomial dependence) holds for complexity 1 systems in the induced arithmetic removal lemma over finite fields remains open as of May 2026. A 2024 follow-up (arXiv:2412.15170) extends the qualitative removal result to all partition-regular patterns of complexity 1 but does not address the polynomial quantitative bound and inherits tower-type bounds from Green's arithmetic regularity lemma. No paper establishing or refuting polynomial dependence for the general complexity 1 case was found in the literature.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We prove an arithmetic analog of the induced graph removal lemma for complexity 1 patterns over finite fields. Informally speaking, we show that given a fixed collection of $r$-colored complexity 1 arithmetic patterns over $\mathbb F_q$, every coloring $\phi \colon \mathbb F_q^n \setminus\{0\} \to [r]$ with $o(1)$ density of every such pattern can be recolored on an $o(1)$-fraction of the space so that no such pattern remains.
