 The conjecture that p-entropic matroids are F_p-representable (linear) for every prime p remains open as of 2026. The source paper proved this for p=2 and p=3, and ruled out linearity for p=9 (a composite), but no follow-up work resolving the question for larger primes (p=5, 7, 11, ...) was found in the literature. The related 2022 paper by Kühne and Yashfe (arXiv:2206.03465) on entropic and almost-multilinear representability of matroids touches adjacent territory but does not appear to settle this specific conjecture.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found that resolves the conjecture for primes p > 3. Context

The paper proves that $p$-entropic matroids are $\mathbb{F}_p$-representable for $p=2$ and $p=3$, and shows they are not linear for $p=9$ (a composite). The central remaining open problem is whether $p$-entropic matroids are always $\mathbb{F}_p$-representable when $p$ is prime, with only partial cases resolved in this work.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:This paper investigates entropic matroids, that is, matroids whose rank function is given as the Shannon entropy of random variables. In particular, we consider $p$-entropic matroids, for which the random variables each have support of cardinality $p$. We draw connections between such entropic matroids and secret-sharing matroids and show that entropic matroids are linear matroids when $p = 2,3$ but not when $p = 9$. Our results leave open the possibility for $p$-entropic matroids to be linear whenever $p$ is prime, with particular cases proved here. Applications of entropic matroids to coding theory and cryptography are also discussed.
