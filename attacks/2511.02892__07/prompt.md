 Problem 7.1 from arXiv:2511.02892 conjectures that every bicritical snark is almost-\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{2}-connected and almost-\mathbb{Z}_{4}-connected. The property has been verified computationally for the Petersen graph and both Blanuša snarks on 18 vertices using the House of Graphs snark database, but no proof for the general case has appeared. Conjecture. Every bicritical snark is almost-$\mathbb{Z}_{2}^{2}$-connected and almost-$\mathbb{Z}_{4}$-connected.

Context

A graph is almost-$A$-connected if all boundaries are admissible for group $A$ except $\partial f=0$. The property has been verified for the Petersen graph and both Blanuša snarks on 18 vertices. The conjecture emerged from exploration of the House of Graphs snark database.
