Positive square energy ≥ n for large clique — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 Conjecture 9.4 from arXiv:2506.07264 asserts that s^+(G) >= n for every connected graph G of order n with clique number omega(G) >= 3; this refines Conjecture 1.1 (s^+(G) >= n-1 for all connected G) by exploiting the presence of a large clique. No follow-up paper resolving or partially settling this specific conjecture was found in a wide web search conducted in May 2026, approximately 11 months after the paper appeared. The closest related work (arXiv:2409.18220, EJC v32i3p53) establishes s(G) >= 3n/4 for all connected G of order n >= 4 but predates 2506.07264 and does not address the clique-number condition.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found. The conjecture is very recent (June 2025). The source paper itself proves the analogous bound for claw-free graphs and diameter-2 graphs (as part of the broader Conjecture 1.2 programme) but leaves Conjecture 9.4 open, identifying unicyclic graphs containing a triangle as the main obstacle. The earlier companion paper arXiv:2409.18220 (same group) proves s(G) >= 3n/4 but is a distinct, weaker result that predates this conjecture.

Conjecture. For any connected graph $G$ of order $n$ and $\omega(G)\geq 3$, we have $s^{+}(G)\geq n$.

Context

This conjecture refines Conjecture 1.1 for graphs with clique number at least 3, motivated by the expectation that a large clique forces a large value of $s^{+}$. It would follow from the truth of Conjectures 1.2 and 9.1; the authors identify unicyclic graphs with a triangle as the main obstacle.

Source paper

 Refinement of a conjecture on positive square energy of graphs
 Saieed Akbari, Hitesh Kumar, Bojan Mohar, Shivaramakrishna Pragada, Shengtong Zhang · 2025-06-08
 https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07264

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Let $G$ be a simple graph of order $n$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_1(G)\geq \cdots \geq \lambda_n(G)$. Define \[s^+(G)=\sum_{\lambda_i >0} \lambda_i^2(G), \quad s^-(G)=\sum_{\lambda_i<0} \lambda_i^2(G).\] It was conjectured by Elphick, Farber, Goldberg and Wocjan that for every connected graph $G$ of order $n$, $s^+(G) \ge n-1.$ We verify this conjecture for graphs with domination number at most 2. We then strengthen the conjecture as follows: if $G$ is a connected graph of order $n$ and size $m \geq n+1$, then $s^+(G) \geq n$. We prove this conjecture for claw-free graphs and graphs with diameter 2.
