Square energy sign in unicyclic odd-cycle graphs — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 Conjecture 9.1 from arXiv:2506.07264 proposes that for a unicyclic graph of order n whose unique cycle has odd length k, the sign of the inequalities between s⁺(G), n, and s⁻(G) is determined by k mod 4. No follow-up paper resolving or partially proving this conjecture was found in a web search conducted in May 2026. The closely related arXiv:2604.15656 (April 2026) concerns p-energies for p=3 (cube of eigenvalues) rather than p=2, and does not address this conjecture.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found. The conjecture is very recent (June 2025). Conjecture. If $G$ is a unicyclic graph of order $n$ whose cycle has odd length $k$, then $s^{+}(G)>n>s^{-}(G)$, if $k\equiv 3\mod 4$. $s^{+}(G)<n<s^{-}(G)$, if $k\equiv 1\mod 4$.

Context

The authors compute $s^{+}$ and $s^{-}$ for odd cycles $C_n$ explicitly and observe a parity-of-$n$-mod-4 dependence. They extend this observation to all unicyclic graphs whose unique cycle has odd length and propose this conjecture.

 Refinement of a conjecture on positive square energy of graphs
 Saieed Akbari, Hitesh Kumar, Bojan Mohar, Shivaramakrishna Pragada, Shengtong Zhang · 2025-06-08
 https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07264

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:Let $G$ be a simple graph of order $n$ with eigenvalues $\lambda_1(G)\geq \cdots \geq \lambda_n(G)$. Define \[s^+(G)=\sum_{\lambda_i >0} \lambda_i^2(G), \quad s^-(G)=\sum_{\lambda_i<0} \lambda_i^2(G).\] It was conjectured by Elphick, Farber, Goldberg and Wocjan that for every connected graph $G$ of order $n$, $s^+(G) \ge n-1.$ We verify this conjecture for graphs with domination number at most 2. We then strengthen the conjecture as follows: if $G$ is a connected graph of order $n$ and size $m \geq n+1$, then $s^+(G) \geq n$. We prove this conjecture for claw-free graphs and graphs with diameter 2.
