 Problem 1.2 asks to determine the complete set of obstructions for diplanar quartic Eulerian digraphs under the partial order defined by cycle removal and suppression of degree-2 vertices. The source paper (Archdeacon, Bonnington, Mohar, AJC 67, 2017) makes partial progress by classifying all obstructions that contain parallel arcs, but the complete list remains open. A related 2022 AJC paper on directed embeddings of 2-regular diplanar digraphs was found but concerns a different subproblem (counting embeddings) and could not be verified as addressing Problem 1.2. No complete resolution was found in the indexed literature.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up paper resolving the complete obstruction set for diplanar quartic Eulerian digraphs was found. A 2022 AJC paper (vol. 82, pp. 256-267) on 'Directed embeddings of 2-regular diplanar digraphs' appeared in searches but its PDF was unreadable (binary) and from available metadata appears to address counting directed embeddings of 2-regular diplanar digraphs rather than classifying obstructions for quartic Eulerian digraphs. The problem is from 2017 and the area is highly specialized, so confidence is medium rather than high.
