Title: Determine the order of f_k(N; s, t) in general
Determine the order of $f_k(N; s, t)$ for all valid parameters $k \ge 3$, $s \ge k+1$, and $1 \le t \le \binom{s}{k}$.

Context:
Despite substantial progress on the qualitative phase-transition structure of $f_k(N;s,t)$ as $t$ varies (the Erdős–Hajnal threshold conjectures, now resolved), the exact order of $f_k(N;s,t)$ has remained wide open. Theorem 1.6 determines the order $f_3(N;s,t) = \Theta(\log N/\log\log N)$ for a constant fraction of $t$-values in the range $(e(s), E(s)]$ for each fixed $s \ge 4$.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We give a probabilistic construction of a $3$-uniform hypergraph on $N$ vertices with independence number $O(\log N / \log \log N)$ in which there are at most two edges among any four vertices. This bound is tight and solves a longstanding open problem of Erdős and Hajnal in Ramsey theory. We further extend this result to prove tight bounds on various other hypergraph Ramsey numbers.
