 The conjecture asks whether, for every triangle-free graph F, one can find graphs G of arbitrarily large chromatic number with the same girth as F in which every F-free induced subgraph is c_F-colourable, strengthening Theorem 3 of the source paper (which only guarantees G is triangle-free, not that it matches the girth of F). The paper itself proves the weaker analogue where girth is replaced by odd girth (Theorem 4), but the full girth version remains unresolved. No subsequent work proving or disproving the conjecture was found in a wide search of the literature through May 2026.

 Reviewer notes. No follow-up found. The partial result in the source paper (Theorem 4) replaces girth with odd girth, which is weaker. The conjecture is recent (posted 2022, published 2023 in Combinatorica) and the absence of follow-up is consistent with it being genuinely open.

Context

Theorem 3 guarantees, for triangle-free $F$, a triangle-free $G$, but does not control the girth of $G$. The authors ask whether $G$ can be taken to have the same girth as $F$, conjecture that this is so, but are unable to prove it. As a partial result they prove Theorem 4, the weaker analogue where girth is replaced by odd girth.

=== Source paper abstract / header ===
Abstract:We prove that, for every graph $F$ with at least one edge, there is a constant $c_F$ such that there are graphs of arbitrarily large chromatic number and the same clique number as $F$ in which every $F$-free induced subgraph has chromatic number at most $c_F$. This generalises recent theorems of Briański, Davies and Walczak, and Carbonero, Hompe, Moore and Spirkl. Our results imply that for every $r\geq 3$ the class of $K_r$-free graphs has a very strong vertex Ramsey-type property, giving a vast generalisation of a result of Folkman from 1970. We also prove related results for tournaments, hypergraphs and infinite families of graphs, and show an analogous statement for graphs where clique number is replaced by odd girth.
