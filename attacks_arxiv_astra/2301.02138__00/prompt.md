Chordal modulator characterization for even-hole-free graphs — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 
 Status
 partial
 high confidence
 

 The conjecture that every chordal graph of clique number at most three modulates even-hole-free graphs (the converse of Theorem 1.2 of Sintiari–Trotignon) remains open in full generality. The source paper itself proved the case when H is a forest (Theorem 1.6). Subsequently, Hajebi (2024/2025) proved the conjecture for H = cone(cone(F)) for any forest F and for H a 'crystal' (a graph built from coned double stars), substantially extending the known special cases, but the full statement for all K₄-free chordal H is still open.

 Reviewer notes. The conjecture from 2301.02138 is equivalent to the main Conjecture 1.8 of arXiv:2401.01299 (bounded-treewidth characterization for even-hole-free graphs): 'K₄-free chordal graphs' equals 'chordal graphs of clique number at most three'. arXiv:2401.01299 proves new cases (Theorems 1.11 and 1.12) but the full conjecture remains open. A strengthening (Conjecture 1.14 in 2401.01299) was disproved by Chudnovsky and Trotignon via a new variant of layered wheels, but this does not affect the main conjecture.
