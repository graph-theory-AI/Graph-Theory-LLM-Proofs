4-hole in controlled triangle-free graphs — Graph-theory open problems (arXiv)

 The informal question of whether every (2,φ)-controlled triangle-free graph with sufficiently large chromatic number must contain a 4-hole remains open. Scott and Seymour proved Statement 2.1 for all ℓ ≥ 5 in the source paper, explicitly noting they could not decide the ℓ = 4 case. A thorough web search found no subsequent paper resolving this question, and the sole internal-corpus candidate (arXiv:2306.02364) is about tournament structure and is unrelated.

 Reviewer notes. The conjecture is phrased as an informal question in the paper, not a numbered conjecture. Statement 2.1 (for ℓ ≥ 5) is fully proved; the ℓ = 4 case is singled out as the remaining open instance. A 2021 paper by Davies (arXiv:2104.05907) disproves a different Scott–Seymour conjecture (about induced wheels in triangle-free Burling graphs) but does not address the 4-hole controlled question. No follow-up resolving the ℓ = 4 case was found in any searched source.

Abstract:A hole in a graph is an induced subgraph which is a cycle of length at least four. We prove that for every positive integer k, every triangle-free graph with sufficiently large chromatic number contains holes of k consecutive lengths.
