=== Problem statement (OpenProblemGarden) ===
Title: List colorings of edge-critical graphs
Conjecture Suppose that $ G $ is a $ \Delta $ -edge-critical graph. Suppose that for each edge $ e $ of $ G $ , there is a list $ L(e) $ of $ \Delta $ colors. Then $ G $ is $ L $ -edge-colorable unless all lists are equal to each other.

=== Discussion / context (OpenProblemGarden) ===
(Reproduced from [M].) A graph $ G $ is said to be $ \Delta $ -edge-critical if it is not $ \Delta $ -edge-colorable but every edge-deleted subgraph is $ \Delta $ -edge-colorable. (Here $ \Delta $ is the maximum degree of $ G $ .)
