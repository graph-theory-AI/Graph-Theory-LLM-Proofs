=== Problem statement (OpenProblemGarden) ===
Title: Monochromatic reachability in arc-colored digraphs
Conjecture For every $ k $ , there exists an integer $ f(k) $ such that if $ D $ is a digraph whose arcs are colored with $ k $ colors, then $ D $ has a $ S $ set which is the union of $ f(k) $ stables sets so that every vertex has a monochromatic path to some vertex in $ S $ .

=== Discussion / context (OpenProblemGarden) ===
In the particular case of tournaments (and more generally when the stabilty number of $ D $ is bounded), it has been proved by Bousquet, Lochet, and Thomassé [BLT].

=== Catalog page (statement + literature review) ===
Monochromatic reachability in arc-colored digraphs — Graph-theory open problems

 
 Status
 open
 medium confidence
 

 The general conjecture — that for every $k$-arc-colored digraph there is a set $S$ equal to a union of $f(k)$ stable sets such that every vertex has a monochromatic path to $S$ — remains open. The special cases of tournaments and digraphs with bounded stability number were resolved by Bousquet, Lochet, and Thomassé before the OPG posting date and are cited in the OPG itself. Post-posting work has established lower bounds on $f(n)$ for tournaments and a 'king-serf duo' structural result for tournaments, but no progress on the general digraph case has been found.

 Cited literature (3)

 
 
 
partial King-serf duo by monochromatic paths in k-edge-coloured tournaments
 (2017)
 

 
 Kristóf Bérczi, Attila Joó · Electronic Journal of Combinatorics · arXiv:1705.00896

For any $k$, shows existence of two disjoint sets $K, S$ of combined size at most $\lambda_k$ in every $k$-edge-colored tournament such that every vertex has a monochromatic path of length at most 2 from $K$ to it or from it to $S$, a structural weakening of the main conjecture restricted to tournaments.
 

 
 
partial A lower bound on the size of an absorbing set in an arc-coloured tournament
 (2017)
 

 
 Laurent Beaudou, Luc Devroye, Gena Hahn · arXiv preprint · arXiv:1708.08891

Establishes a lower bound on $f(n)$, the minimum size of a monochromatic-path absorbing set in $n$-arc-colored tournaments, complementing the upper bound from the BLT proof.
 

 
 
partial A proof of the Erdős-Sands-Sauer-Woodrow conjecture
 (2017)
 

 
 N. Bousquet, W. Lochet, S. Thomassé · arXiv preprint · arXiv:1703.08123

Proves the conjecture for the special case of digraphs with bounded stability number, using the fact that the fractional domination of vertices by stable sets is at most 2 when the stability number is bounded.
