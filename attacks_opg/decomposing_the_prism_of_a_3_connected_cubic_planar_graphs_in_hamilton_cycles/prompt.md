=== Problem statement (OpenProblemGarden) ===
Title: Hamilton decomposition of prisms over 3-connected cubic planar graphs
Conjecture Every prism over a $ 3 $ -connected cubic planar graph can be decomposed into two Hamilton cycles.

=== Discussion / context (OpenProblemGarden) ===
The prism over a graph $ G $ is the Cartesian product $ G\square K_2 $ . Rosenfeld and Barnette [RB] deduced from the Four-Colour Theorem that the prism over cubic planar 3-connected $ G $ has a Hamilton cycle $ C $ . The graph $ G\setminus E(C) $ is cycle factor (spanning union of cycles). The conjecture says that one can choses $ C $ so that the cycle factor $ G\setminus E(C) $ has a unique cycle, that is a Hamilton cycle.

Hamilton decomposition of prisms over 3-connected cubic planar graphs — Graph-theory open problems

 
 Status
 partial
 high confidence
 

 The Alspach–Rosenfeld conjecture that every prism $G\square K_2$ over a 3-connected cubic planar graph $G$ decomposes into two Hamilton cycles remains open in full generality. Significant partial results were established before the posting (bipartite planar cubic graphs, duals of kleetopes). After the 2013 posting, Rosenfeld and Xiang (2015) extended the family of confirmed cases to include prisms over cubic Halin graphs, cubic generalized Halin graphs of order $4k+2$, and several further infinite families.
