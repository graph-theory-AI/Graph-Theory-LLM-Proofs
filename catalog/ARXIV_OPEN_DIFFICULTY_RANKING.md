The ranking is a triage heuristic, not a theorem and not a probability estimate.

## Rubric

- `Difficulty`: 1-5, where 5 means broad/classical/landmark-level or tied to a major unresolved programme.
- `Lean`: the more plausible successful direction: `prove` means the statement/positive answer looks more plausible; `disprove` means counterexample/negative answer looks more plausible; `balanced` means neither side is clearly favoured.
- Signals used: status (`open` vs `partial`), age, named conjecture language, breadth of quantifiers, hard-domain keywords, equivalences/implications, and review text such as “wide open” or “full generality”.
- I spot-checked the highest tier against current arXiv pages on 2026-05-16, but this is not a fresh web review of all 686 records.
