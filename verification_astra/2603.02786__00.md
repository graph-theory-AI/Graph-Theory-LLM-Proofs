# Referee report: 2603.02786__00 (leg `attacks_retry`)

**Target claim.** $m(n)=m_{[n]}(n)\sim\frac43\cdot\frac{n^{3/2}}{\ln n}$, i.e. the
leading-constant conjecture of Alon, Dębski, Grytczuk and Przybyło
(arXiv:2603.02786, Theorem 1 gives $4/3$ and $5/3$) for the *full* difference set
$\{A_d=D_d\cap[n]:d\in[n]\}$.

I went in expecting to find the error. I did not find one. What I found instead is a
**priority problem**: the hard, constant-determining half of the argument was posted
to arXiv by an independent group nine days before this attack ran.

## Interpretation

Checked against `prompt.md` and against the source paper's own notation (via
arXiv:2609.07487 §1, which restates it verbatim).

* $A_d(n)=\{d,2d,\ldots,\lfloor n/d\rfloor d\}$, integer shifts, packing length =
  number of integer points of the host interval. The writeup uses exactly this and
  notes (correctly) that cardinality vs. diameter shifts everything by $O(1)$.
* $\log$ is natural throughout (the writeup uses $\log n=2\log x$ together with the
  PNT), matching the conjecture's $\ln n$.
* The theorem proved is the **full** conjecture $m_{[n]}(n)\sim\frac43T_n$, not a
  prime-restricted or otherwise weakened surrogate. Section 7 explicitly covers every
  index $d\le n$, and I verified that cover exhaustively by computer (below). There
  is no interpretation gaming: an author of the original problem would consider this
  the intended statement.
* The claim is *asymptotic only*; nothing about a rate is claimed, and the writeup
  says so. That matches the conjecture's "$\sim$".

The retry prompt contained the earlier `gpt-5.6-sol` attempt (verdict `partial`,
prime subfamily only, upper constant $(8\sqrt2-7)/3$). The new writeup does **not**
inherit it uncritically: it reproves the separation lemma from scratch in §1 (I
checked that proof independently) and it explicitly discards the path/triple
condition (3.2) that stalled the earlier attempt. It does not reproduce the earlier
attempt's limitation.

## Reference check

The writeup invokes **no external theorem at all** except the prime number theorem
and elementary divisor/CRT facts, all of which I re-derived. So there is no misquoted
citation to catch. That, however, is also the problem:

**Priority (step 2b).** I found an independent preprint that already contains the
constant-determining half of this argument.

* **arXiv:2609.07487**, *Asymptotically optimal packings of arithmetic progressions
  with prime differences*, Jianfeng Hou, Siyue Liu, Hongbin Zhao (Center for Discrete
  Mathematics, Fuzhou University), submitted **7 Sep 2026** — nine days before this
  attack ran (16 Sep 2026). Theorem 1.1:
  $m_{\mathcal P(\sqrt n)}(n)=\left(\frac43+o(1)\right)\frac{n^{3/2}}{\ln n}$.
  Their method: "cyclic phase selection" (Lemma 2.2 there is the same
  interval-vs-CRT-period avoidance the writeup uses) plus §3 "**Regular prime
  blocks**", which partitions primes in $(P,(1+\eta)P]$ into good transversals of
  fixed size $s\ge3$ by exactly the writeup's criterion — no nonzero $h$ with
  $\|h\|_2<R$ satisfying $\sum h_ip_i^{-1}\equiv0\pmod r$. This is the writeup's
  Lemma 3.1 in different clothing (they get simultaneity from Banaszczyk's
  transference inequality where the writeup uses a Weyl-sum/bump-function argument).
* **This is not self-contamination (step 2c).** I opened the actual arXiv abstract
  page and the full HTML: real authors with institutional e-mail addresses, a Chinese
  NKRDP grant number, a 12-item bibliography (Alon–Bóna–Spencer, Banaszczyk,
  Coven–Meyerowitz, Ford, Green–Ruzsa, Katz–Tao, Montgomery–Vaughan, …), and an
  independent Semantic Scholar record (CorpusId 291870588, 0 citations). Nothing
  traces back to this repository.
* **Crucially, it does not cover the target.** Their §6 Concluding remarks state
  plainly: "A direct extension of the present method to arbitrary differences would
  have to account for nontrivial common divisors. For example, the differences
  $p,2p,\ldots$ are not pairwise coprime, so the modular inverses used in the current
  phase parameterization are unavailable. … obtaining the same leading constants for
  the full difference set $[n]$ remains a further problem." That is precisely the
  obstacle the writeup's §2 (bundle colouring into $o(p)$ residue classes) + §4 (Bohr
  set clustering) + §7 (cover) remove. Nothing cites 2609.07487 yet.

So: **the claimed theorem is not in the literature; its load-bearing prime-range
lemma is.** The writeup nowhere acknowledges this (it could not have — it cites
nothing), and a referee would require the overlap to be stated.

Two neighbouring facts worth recording for the sibling reviews:

* **arXiv:2607.06113** (Mao, Wang, Wei, Yang, 7 Jul 2026) proves
  $M_k(n)=(1+o(1))nk$ for fixed $n$, settling Conjecture 7 of 2603.02786. The
  sibling target `2603.02786__04` claims exactly "$M_k(n)=nk+o(k)$ for every fixed
  $n$" — that looks like the same theorem and should be checked for ALREADY_KNOWN
  first.
* The source paper's own $5/3$ upper bound corresponds to capturing only half the
  pairwise overlap saving: $2\int_0^1(1-u^2/2)\,du=5/3$, against
  $2\int_0^1(1-u^2)\,du=4/3$ for the full saving. I verified both integrals. This
  confirms that the writeup is attacking the right mechanism rather than an artefact.

## Caveats

* **Priority.** The prime-range half — Lemma 3.1 and the whole of §5, which is where
  the constant $4/3$ is actually produced — duplicates Theorem 1.1 of
  arXiv:2609.07487 (7 Sep 2026). Genuinely new here is only §2 + §4 + §6 + §7: the
  reduction of a whole bundle $\{A_{ap}\}$ to $o(p)$ residue classes and their
  clustering inside one Bohr set. That is a real idea and it answers the exact
  question Hou–Liu–Zhao left open — but any write-up must say so.
* **Wholly non-effective.** $K_0$ (divisor bound), $H(k,\eta)$ (Fourier cutoff),
  $L(k,a,b,\eta)$ (exceptional primes), $c(d,\rho)=M^{-d}$ (Bohr density) are all
  unquantified and, where one can estimate them, enormous. Combined with the
  measured convergence (1.52 at $n=10^{10}$), the result is qualitative in the
  strongest sense.
* **Smooth-bump support** (step 11) must be shrunk below $\eta$ for the open-arc
  conclusion; trivial.
* **$\nu<\epsilon^2/4$** is imposed in §5.1 but never actually invoked; it is only
  needed to keep $W-g$ and the complement positive, which (5.2) already gives.
* **Lemma 3.1's genericity is fragile in a way the text hides** (step 12b): every
  triple is bad at frequency $\sim p_{\max}-p_{\min}$, so the lemma lives entirely on
  the fixed-$H$/growing-$X$ separation. The proof handles it; the exposition does
  not flag it, and a reader could easily believe good blocks are the rule for
  trivial reasons.
* **Deletion of duplicates** (indices with two prime factors in $[\epsilon x,Cx]$)
  is asserted in one sentence; it is correct (removing sets from a packing keeps it a
  packing) but is the only place where the "pack labelled copies" device is used.
* The writeup contains **no bibliography**, so "references_ok" above means "no
  citation is misused", not "the literature was consulted".

## Is arXiv:2603.02786 easy, or is one trick being reapplied?

Asked because three of its sub-problems were claimed in this batch and a fourth was
confirmed earlier. My reading: **mostly genuinely tractable, with one shared
mechanism.**

The paper is six months old and its conjecture list is being picked off by
independent human groups at speed — Conjecture 7 by Mao–Wang–Wei–Yang in July
(arXiv:2607.06113), Conjectures 3 and 4 by Hou–Liu–Zhao in September
(arXiv:2609.07487). These are open problems at the frontier of routine technique, not
deep ones; the authors themselves only said "additional ideas" were needed. So a
strong model getting traction on several of them at once is not suspicious per se.

There *is* a recurring trick, though, and it is the CRT one: control the residue
class (phase) of each progression modulo its own difference, and use coprimality of
distinct prime differences to make the intersection pattern of two progressions
depend only on one phase difference mod $pq$. `__03` (confirmed earlier) uses it in
lower-bound form (cores with distinct phases mod $\gcd$); `__00` uses it in
upper-bound form. That is the paper's native technique, so its recurrence is
expected rather than evidence of a template being over-applied. What I would *not*
assume from this target is that the siblings are equally sound: `__04`'s stated
conclusion ($M_k(n)=nk+o(k)$ for fixed $n$) appears to be literally the published
theorem of arXiv:2607.06113, and `__01`'s own verdict block admits novelty was never
checked. Both deserve a priority check before a correctness check.

## Referee summary

I attacked this writeup for a full pass and could not break it. Every one of the 24
numbered steps re-derives correctly; the two defects I found (the bump function's
support, the glib divisor-bound constant) are one-line repairs, and the one real
subtlety — that *every* prime triple satisfies a CRT relation, so Lemma 3.1 depends
entirely on the fixed frequency cutoff $H$ against growing prime gaps — is handled
correctly by the bad-tuple count even though the text never draws attention to it. I
instantiated the whole §5.1 block construction literally at $n=10^8$ with three
mutually overlapping host windows and all composite-indexed progressions present, and
it produced 172 850 pairwise-distinct points inside the claimed span; Corollary 2.2,
Lemmas 6.1 and 6.2, and the §7 cover all pass independent tests. The mechanism that
answers the conjecture — pushing a whole bundle $\{A_{ap}(n)\}_a$ into $o(p)$ residue
classes that all sit inside one Bohr set, so that cross-bundle disjointness is
decided purely by the coprime pair $(p_i,p_j)$ — is correct and is exactly the device
that Hou–Liu–Zhao name as the missing ingredient for general differences. What sinks
the "would_publish: true" claim is not an error but the calendar: arXiv:2609.07487,
posted nine days before the attack, already proves the prime-difference case
$m_{\mathcal P(\sqrt n)}(n)\sim\frac43n^{3/2}/\ln n$ by the same regular-prime-block
route, so the genuinely new content is the composite extension, not the constant. I
record MINOR_GAPS rather than CONFIRMED because of the two cosmetic repairs and
because the limiting regime that actually delivers $4/3$ is beyond computational
reach (at $n=10^{10}$ the lower-bound expression is still 1.52), and medium
confidence because a claim of this size deserves a second human pass on §5.2's
interchange of limits.
